# CHIM lights roadmap

How CHIM gets every exterior cell lit like the original, step by step. The design and the measurements behind each
step are in [CHIM lighting](LIGHTING.md). Progress per cell is in the [cell tracker](CELL_TRACKER.md): a cell whose
only missing criterion is light shows as "awaiting lighting" (zebra) and turns solid green when its lighting audit
says lit.

Priority (owner, 9 October 2026): the open world goes in first. Lighting work that would slow the world conversion
waits; the builds and bakes of steps 3 to 9 are queued until then.

The builder option is `--chim-lighting-type`, default `hybrid` (owner decision, 9 October 2026):

| Type | What it does | State |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `none` | No light sources: constant terrain light, models without lightmaps, no night lamps | available |
| `lamps` | The v0.0.33 lighting: constant terrain light, the night lamp table (lamps, lanterns, torches, fires, candles) | available |
| `hybrid` | Terrain lightmaps, one light level per placed model, the nearest sources of every class as dynamic lights, per-plant glow | default; parts land step by step below |
| `baked-e` | `hybrid` plus per-placement lightmaps where a light reaches | reserved: later, for an increased-memory version of the game |
| `full` | Per-placement lightmaps for every placed model | reserved: later, for an increased-memory version of the game |

The reserved types are refused with a message that says why.

## Pending decisions

- **Balmora's ring room** (milestone 8, CHIM-BALMORA-LIGHT-ROOM-33): any stored light needs room made in Balmora's ring.
  The options are a smaller hysteresis or draw distance in Balmora, lighter house models, or a larger CHIM zone.
  Pending with the owner. The first slice is built on Seyda Neen and one open-world ring until then.
- **The increased-memory version** (milestones 7 and later): whether and when `baked-e` and `full` get a build with
  a larger CHIM zone. Pending.

## How "done" is checked

- **The tracker's lighting audit** (`tools/cell_lighting.py`): the cell says lit. Every light source is baked or
  otherwise represented, and every terrain and model surface carries real light data.
- **The OpenMW A/B**: the same poses in OpenMW and in FS-UAE, at noon and at night.
  - Headlamp off frames are the only lighting evidence.
  - Headlamp on frames are for geometry.
  - The test areas: Balmora streets with lamps, the Bitter Coast's glowing plants, a darkener where one is in range.
- **The heap audit**: the CHIM heap gate passes with the lighting bytes in the ring, and the renderer counters
  (entities sent, faces drawn, surface-cache builds) do not get worse on the benchmark cameras.
