## Fast RAM

Every preset starts with 16 MiB of Zorro III Fast RAM (the default, unchanged).
The playtest launcher offers more: 128 MiB (like a 68060 accelerator card),
256 MiB, 512 MiB, 1 GiB, 1.5 GiB (the most FS-UAE 3.1.66 and WinUAE give) or a
custom size, checked against what the emulator can make. Sizes, limits and the
keys written: [Fast RAM](../../FS-UAE-LAUNCHER.md#fast-ram).
