# Every calculation the game makes

_Last updated with AmiWind v0.0.61-dev3 (2026-11-06)._

The rules behind every roll, price, speed and stat of a being, written in words from OpenMW 0.51's source (GPL-3.0; read
for the rules only, nothing copied) and its data. Each calculation lists its inputs (the stats of the beings and the game
settings by their names, with the values the game data gives; Morrowind, Tribunal and Bloodmoon agree on all of them),
where OpenMW does it (file and function under apps/openmw, or files/data-mw for the parts it scripts), and how AmiWind
stands: **in** (coded, with the engine file), **partial** (some of it is coded) or **not yet**. The values a being holds
are on the page [ACTOR_RECORDS.md](ACTOR_RECORDS.md); how the pieces are tracked is on [GAME_FEATURES.md](GAME_FEATURES.md).
Where the original game differs from OpenMW the note says so; OpenMW follows the original unless stated.

## Terms used throughout

- **Roll 0 to 99** means a whole number from 0 to 99, each equally likely; "the roll passes" means it is below the chance
  (some rules say at most or at least, as named). **Chance** values are percentages and are cut to 0..100 where named.
- **The fatigue term** is how much tiredness changes a roll: the game setting fFatigueBase (1.25) minus fFatigueMult (0.5)
  times (1 minus current fatigue divided by maximum fatigue). The ratio is never below 0; a being with a maximum of 0
  counts as full. So a rested being multiplies its roll by 1.25 and an exhausted one by 0.75. It is in nearly every roll
  below (OpenMW: mwmechanics/creaturestats.cpp getFatigueTerm). AmiWind: **in** (aw_combat_rules.c AW_CombatFatigueTerm,
  aw_move_rules.c AW_MoveFatigueTerm).
- **Skill** of a creature is its combat, magic or stealth value by the skill's specialization (see ACTOR_RECORDS.md).
- **Normalized encumbrance** is the carried weight divided by the carrying capacity (below); above 1 the being is overloaded.
- Skill, attribute and effect names are the original's. A "Fortify", "Resist" or "Weakness" magnitude is the total magnitude
  of that effect on the being at that moment.
- All engine file names are under engine/aga/src.

## Melee and ranged combat

| Calculation | How it works | OpenMW 0.51 | AmiWind |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Evasion (defence) | A fifth of Agility plus a tenth of Luck, times the defender's fatigue term, plus the Sanctuary magnitude (at most 100). | mwmechanics/creaturestats.cpp getEvasion | **partial**: the attribute and fatigue part is in (aw_combat_rules.c); Sanctuary is not yet |
| Hit chance (melee and ranged) | The attacker's term is (weapon skill plus a fifth of Agility plus a tenth of Luck) times the attacker's fatigue term, plus Fortify Attack, minus Blind. The defender's term is evasion, but 0 when the defender is knocked down, paralysed, or (for the player's attack) has not noticed the player and is not fighting; plus fCombatInvisoMult (0.2) times the Chameleon magnitude and the same times the Invisibility magnitude, each part at most 100. A defender with fatigue below 0 has no defence. The chance is the attacker's term minus the defender's, rounded. A roll 0 to 99 below the chance hits. The weapon skill is the weapon's own (Long Blade, Axe, Marksman and the like), or Hand-to-hand for fists. | mwmechanics/combat.cpp getHitChance; mwclass/npc.cpp hit | **in** (aw_combat_rules.c AW_CombatHitChance, AW_CombatSwing); Chameleon, Invisibility, Fortify Attack and Blind are not yet |
| Melee weapon damage | A weapon has a lowest and a highest damage for each of three attacks (chop, slash, thrust). The damage is the lowest plus (highest minus lowest) times the attack strength (0 for a tap, 1 for a full swing). It is multiplied by the weapon's condition (current divided by maximum) and by fDamageStrengthBase (0.5) plus a tenth of fDamageStrengthMult (0.1) times Strength, which is 0.5 plus 0.01 per point of Strength. | mwclass/npc.cpp hit; mwmechanics/combat.cpp adjustWeaponDamage | **in** (aw_combat_rules.c AW_CombatSwing) |
| Hand-to-hand damage | The Hand-to-hand skill times (fMinHandToHandMult 0.1 plus (fMaxHandToHandMult 0.5 minus 0.1) times the attack strength). The blow hits the victim's fatigue; it hits health instead (at fHandtoHandHealthPer, 0.1, of that amount) when the victim is knocked down or paralysed or the attacker is a werewolf. A setting can also scale it by Strength divided by 40. A werewolf's claws multiply it by the game's werewolf claw global. | mwmechanics/combat.cpp getHandToHandDamage | **in** (aw_combat_rules.c); the Strength option and werewolf form are not yet |
| Ranged damage | A bow or crossbow adds its own lowest-to-highest chop damage, taken by how far it was drawn, to the arrow's or bolt's damage taken the same way. A thrown weapon counts twice. The sum is then adjusted as melee damage. The hit chance uses the weapon's skill (Marksman). A shot that hits an enemy has a chance of fProjectileThrownStoreChance (25) per cent to end up in its inventory when not enchanted. | mwmechanics/combat.cpp projectileHit | **not yet** (no bows, arrows or thrown weapons fire) |
| Unaware and knocked-down bonus | When the player strikes a being that is not fighting and has not noticed the player, melee damage is multiplied by fCombatCriticalStrikeMult (4). Damage against a knocked-down being is multiplied by fCombatKODamageMult (1.5); ranged shots use the 1.5 for both cases. | mwclass/npc.cpp hit; mwmechanics/combat.cpp projectileHit | **in** (aw_combat_rules.c AW_CombatSwing: the victim's aware flag and the knocked-down state); what makes a being unaware belongs to the sneaking work (not yet) |
| Resist and weaken normal weapons, silver | A weapon with no Silver flag, no magic flag (and no enchantment, by default) is a normal weapon. Its damage is multiplied by 1 minus the smaller of 1 and (Resist Normal Weapons minus Weakness to Normal Weapons, as a fraction). A silver weapon against a werewolf is multiplied by fWereWolfSilverWeaponDamageMult (2). | mwmechanics/combat.cpp resistNormalWeapon, applyWerewolfDamageMult | **in** for ordinary weapons (v0.0.61: the combat code reads Resist and Weakness to Normal Weapons from the active effects, [COMBAT.md](COMBAT.md)); the silver weapon against a werewolf is not yet |
| Armour rating of a being | Each worn piece has a rating: its base armour times the wearer's skill in that armour (Light, Medium or Heavy) divided by iBaseArmorSkill (30), times its condition; a piece with zero weight keeps its base armour. The skill class comes from the piece's weight against the slot's weight setting (boots iBootsWeight 20, cuirass iCuirassWeight 30, greaves iGreavesWeight 15, helmet iHelmWeight 5, gauntlet and bracer iGauntletWeight 5, pauldron iPauldronWeight 10, shield iShieldWeight 15): up to fLightMaxMod (0.6) of it is light, up to fMedMaxMod (0.9) is medium, above that heavy. An empty slot counts as (fUnarmoredBase1 0.1 times Unarmored) times (fUnarmoredBase2 0.065 times Unarmored). The whole rating is the cuirass times 0.3, plus 0.1 times each of the shield, helmet, greaves, boots and the two pauldrons, plus 0.05 times each gauntlet, plus the Shield effect magnitude. A creature has only the Shield magnitude. | files/data-mw/scripts/omw/combat/local.lua getArmorRating, getSkillAdjustedArmorRating, getArmorSkill; mwclass/armor.cpp; mwclass/npc.cpp getArmorRating | **in** (aw_combat_rules.c, the rating of a sheet; the worn pieces from aw_combat.c; the inventory window shows it as "Armor: N" and adds the Shield effect there); the Shield effect does not yet reduce the blows that land (`COMBAT-SHIELD-EFFECT-NO-ARMOR-61`) |
| Damage reduction by armour | Health damage is multiplied by the larger of (damage divided by (damage plus armour rating)) and fCombatArmorMinMult (0.25), never below 1 point. A roll 0 to 99 chooses the piece struck: 0 to 29 cuirass, 30 to 39 helmet, 40 to 49 greaves, 50 to 59 boots, 60 to 69 left pauldron, 70 to 79 right pauldron, 80 to 84 left gauntlet, 85 to 89 right gauntlet, 90 to 99 shield. The piece struck loses condition equal to the damage the armour absorbed. | files/data-mw/scripts/omw/combat/local.lua adjustDamageForArmor, pickRandomArmor, applyArmor | **in** (aw_combat_rules.c AW_CombatArmorHitSlot, AW_CombatSwing) |
| Block | Possible only for a being with a shield, not knocked down, not recovering from a hit, not paralysed, with the attacker within the angle from fCombatBlockLeftAngle (-90 degrees) to fCombatBlockRightAngle (30 degrees) of its facing. The blocker's term is (Block plus a fifth of Agility plus a tenth of Luck) times (swing strength times fSwingBlockMult 1 plus fSwingBlockBase 1), times fBlockStillBonus (1.25) when not moving forward, times its fatigue term. The attacker's term is (weapon skill plus a fifth of Agility plus a tenth of Luck) times its fatigue term. The chance is the blocker's term minus the attacker's, cut to the range from iBlockMinChance (10) to iBlockMaxChance (50). A roll 0 to 99 below it blocks: the damage becomes 0, the shield loses condition equal to the damage, and the blocker loses fatigue: fFatigueBlockBase (4) plus normalized encumbrance times fFatigueBlockMult (0) plus the attacker's weapon weight times the swing times fWeaponFatigueBlockMult (1). | mwmechanics/combat.cpp blockMeleeAttack | **in** (aw_combat_rules.c AW_CombatCanBlock, AW_CombatSwing) |
| Knockdown and stagger | When a hit does health damage of at least Agility times fKnockDownMult (0.5), and a roll 0 to 99 is at least iKnockDownOddsBase (50) plus Agility times iKnockDownOddsMult (50) divided by 100, the victim is knocked down (so Agility 100 is never knocked down); otherwise it staggers (cannot block for a moment). Fatigue at or below 0 knocks a being out. | mwclass/npc.cpp onHit | **in** (aw_combat_rules.c) |
| Weapon and shield wear | Every landed blow costs the weapon the larger of 1 and fWeaponDamageMult (0.1) times the damage; a miss that was in reach costs 1. At 0 the item is taken off. | mwmechanics/combat.cpp reduceWeaponCondition | **in** (aw_combat_rules.c wear) |
| Fatigue cost of a swing | fFatigueAttackBase (2) plus normalized encumbrance times fFatigueAttackMult (0) plus the weapon's weight times the attack strength times fWeaponFatigueMult (0.25). | mwmechanics/combat.cpp applyFatigueLoss | **in** (aw_combat_rules.c) |
| Elemental shields | A being struck while it has Fire, Lightning or Frost Shield makes the attacker take damage: fElementalShieldMult (0.1) times the shield magnitude, reduced by the attacker's resistance and by a save roll based on its Destruction, Willpower and Luck. | mwmechanics/combat.cpp applyElementalShields | **not yet** |
| Difficulty | With difficulty d (the slider from -100 to 100, divided by 100): damage to the player is multiplied by 1 plus fDifficultyMult (5) times d when d is above 0, or 1 plus d divided by 5 when below; damage by the player by 1 minus d divided by 5 when d is above 0, or 1 plus 5 times the size of d when below. | files/data-mw/scripts/omw/combat/local.lua adjustDamageForDifficulty; mwmechanics/difficultyscaling.cpp | **not yet** |

## Magic: casting, effects and resistance

| Calculation | How it works | OpenMW 0.51 | AmiWind |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Spell cost (magicka) | A spell that is not auto-calculated costs its record's value. An auto-calculated one costs the sum over its effects of: half the sum of the lowest and highest magnitude (each at least 1; an effect without magnitude counts 1), times a tenth of the effect's base cost, times the duration (at least 1, unless the effect is applied once; an effect without duration counts 1), plus 0.05 times the area times the base cost, all times fEffectCostMult (0.5), times 1.5 for a target-range effect; each effect at least 0; rounded. | mwmechanics/spellutil.cpp calcSpellCost, calcEffectCost | **in** (tools/mwscript/actors.py spell_cost in the builder; aw_dial.c for prices) |
| Spell cast chance | For each effect the game works out a cost term (as above but with the magnitudes not raised to 1) times fEffectCostMult, 1.5 times for target range, and takes the effect for which two times the skill of the effect's school minus that term is lowest: that is the spell's weakest school. The chance is two times the skill of that school, minus the spell's cost, plus 0.2 times Willpower plus 0.1 times Luck, minus the Sound magnitude, times the fatigue term, cut to 0..100. A spell flagged as always succeeding, and every ability, curse, blight and disease, is 100; a power is 100 once a day and 0 while spent; Silence or too little magicka is 0. A roll 0 to 99 at or above the chance fails the cast. | mwmechanics/spellutil.cpp calcSpellBaseSuccessChance, getSpellSuccessChance; spellcasting.cpp cast | **in** for the player's casts (v0.0.61; aw_cast_rules.c AW_CastBaseChance, AW_CastChance; [MAGIC_EFFECTS.md](MAGIC_EFFECTS.md)) |
| Cost paid for a cast | A successful cast takes the spell cost from magicka and the spell cost times (fFatigueSpellBase 0 plus normalized encumbrance times fFatigueSpellMult 0) from fatigue; the data sets both to 0. A cast trains the weakest school's skill unless it is a power or always succeeds. | mwworld/worldimp.cpp; mwmechanics/spellcasting.cpp | **in** for the player (v0.0.61: the magicka is paid at the start of the cast, a failed roll keeps it, the school's skill is trained by a success; the fatigue part is 0 in the data) |
| Magnitude and duration rolls | An effect has a lowest and highest magnitude; each time it is applied a whole number from the lowest to the highest, each equally likely, is rolled. The duration is the record's, or 1 for an effect without duration. Effects with a duration act every moment of it with the magnitude scaled by the time passed. | mwmechanics/spelleffects.cpp roll; spellcasting.cpp inflict | **in** (v0.0.61: each magnitude is rolled once between its lowest and highest when applied, aw_cast_rules.c AW_CastMagnitude, and the effect pool of aw_effects.c runs it over the duration, kept in saved games) |
| Resistance and weakness | The resisted share of a magnitude is worked out per effect that has a resisting effect (Resist Fire for fire damage, and so on). The base resistance is the Resist magnitude minus the Weakness magnitude, plus the matching shield for fire, shock and frost damage. The target's chance term is (Willpower plus a tenth of Luck) times its fatigue term; when the spell was cast by a being, it is multiplied by 50 divided by the caster's uncapped cast chance. A roll 0 to 100 is made; if the term is not above the roll there is no extra resistance, otherwise the extra resistance is the roll divided by the smaller of the term and 100 (an effect without magnitude is resisted whole instead). The total, at most 100, is a percentage: the magnitude is multiplied by 1 minus it; at 100 the effect is dropped. | mwmechanics/spellresistance.cpp getEffectResistance, getEffectResistanceAttribute; spelleffects.cpp applyProtections | **not yet** for spell effects in general; Resist and Weakness to Normal Weapons are read by the combat code (v0.0.61) |
| Absorb and reflect | When a spell from another being lands, each active Reflect and Spell Absorption effect of the target is tried in turn: a roll 0 to 99 below the effect's magnitude reflects the spell back to its caster (only for effects that can be reflected) or absorbs it (the effects are cancelled). Both are tried before resistance. | mwmechanics/spelleffects.cpp applyProtections; activespells.cpp | **not yet** |
| Fortify, Drain, Damage and Restore of a stat | Fortify raises and Drain lowers an attribute or skill for the duration and is undone at the end; Damage and Restore of health, magicka or fatigue act every second. | mwmechanics/spelleffects.cpp | **partial**: the player's record takes these (aw_effects.c), and the effects of non-player actors run while they are loaded and feed the fight (v0.0.61); an ended Fortify Attribute on the player leaves a drop counted as damage (`PLAYER-FORTIFY-END-AS-DAMAGE-61`) |
| Fortify Maximum Magicka | Maximum magicka is Intelligence times (the base multiplier plus a tenth of the effect's magnitude). | mwmechanics/creaturestats.cpp recalculateMagicka | **not yet** (the racial and birthsign bonus of a tenth per point is in the character sheet) |
| Spell price at a spell seller | The larger of 1 and the spell's cost times fSpellValueMult (10), then the barter offer. | mwgui/spellbuyingwindow.cpp addSpell | **in** (aw_dial.c AW_DialSpellPrice) |
| Spellmaking | The player's own spell costs, for each effect, as the spell cost above but with the duration counted one longer and the area at least 1, each effect at least 1 point, and the running total times 1.5 as each target-range effect is added; the price is the larger of 1 and the cost times fSpellMakingValueMult (7), then the barter offer. | mwgui/spellcreationdialog.cpp; spellutil.cpp calcEffectCost | **partial**: the cost and price rules are in (aw_dial.c AW_DialSpellmaking*); the full feature is not yet proven in game |
| Auto-calculated spells | The spells a person gets from skills, and the player's starting spells: see ACTOR_RECORDS.md "Derived values" (iAutoSpell settings, fAutoSpellChance 80, fAutoPCSpellChance 50). | mwmechanics/autocalcspell.cpp | **in** for the player's starting spells (v0.0.61-dev3: aw_cast_rules.c AW_CastStartSpells, chosen when the character is made; a Breton mage of the Mage sign has 10, a warrior none); the spells a person gets from skills are not yet |

## Enchanting, alchemy, repair and soul gems

| Calculation | How it works | OpenMW 0.51 | AmiWind |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Enchantment cost of effects | Effects are added one after another to a running total: each adds ((lowest plus highest magnitude, each at least 1) times the duration plus the area, at least 1) times the effect's base cost times fEffectCostMult (0.5) times 0.05; the total is raised to at least 1 and multiplied by 1.5 when this effect is target range. For a constant effect the duration is fEnchantmentConstantDurationMult (100). | mwmechanics/enchanting.cpp getEffectCosts, getEnchantPoints | **partial**: the rule is in (aw_dial.c AW_DialEnchantCosts, AW_DialEnchantPoints); the feature is not yet proven in game |
| Capacity of an item | The item's enchantment points (a field of its record) times fEnchantmentMult (0.1). The points of the chosen effects may not exceed it. | mwmechanics/enchanting.cpp getMaxEnchantValue | **partial**: the rule is in (aw_dial.c AW_DialEnchantCapacity) |
| Chance to enchant yourself | (Enchant skill minus the enchantment points times fEnchantmentChanceMult (3) times a multiplier for the cast style times the number of items, plus 0.2 Intelligence plus 0.1 Luck) times the fatigue term, times fEnchantmentConstantChanceMult (0.5) for a constant effect; whole number. | mwmechanics/enchanting.cpp getEnchantChance | **partial**: the rule is in (aw_dial.c AW_DialEnchantChance) |
| Price at an enchanter | The final accumulated effect cost times fEnchantmentValueMult (1000), then the barter offer, times the number of items and the cast-style multiplier. | mwmechanics/enchanting.cpp getEnchantPrice | **partial**: the rule is in (aw_dial.c AW_DialEnchantPrice) |
| Charge of an item | An auto-calculated enchantment holds its rounded effect cost times a number for the cast style: iMagicItemChargeOnce (1), iMagicItemChargeStrike (10), iMagicItemChargeUse (5), iMagicItemChargeConst (10); otherwise the record's charge. A new enchantment's charge is the filled soul's value. | mwmechanics/spellutil.cpp getEnchantmentCharge; enchanting.cpp | **partial**: the charge is read from the record (aw_inventory.c); making one is not yet |
| Cost of using an enchanted item | The enchantment's cast cost lowered by one percent for each point of Enchant skill above 10 (raised for each below), never under 1. A use that cannot be paid fails. | mwmechanics/spellutil.cpp getEffectiveEnchantmentCastCost; spellcasting.cpp cast | **in** (aw_cast_rules.c AW_CastItemCost; v0.0.61-dev3: Cast When Strikes, Cast When Used and scrolls spend charge by it, a use that cannot be paid is refused; Constant Effect items act while worn) |
| Recharge by time | An enchanted item regains fMagicItemRechargePerSecond (0.05) charge each second, up to its maximum. | mwmechanics/recharge.cpp | **in** (aw_inventory.c) |
| Recharge with a soul gem | A chance of (Enchant skill plus the smaller of 20 and 0.2 Intelligence, at least 1, plus a tenth of Luck as 1 to 10) times the fatigue term; a roll 0 to 99 below it succeeds and restores the gem's soul value times the roll divided by the chance. The gem is used up either way and the Enchant skill is trained. | mwmechanics/recharge.cpp rechargeItem | **not yet** |
| Soul trap | A creature killed under Soul Trap puts its soul value (a field of its record) into an empty soul gem the trapper carries. | mwmechanics/spelleffects.cpp Soultrap | **partial**: filled gems are stored (aw_actorstate.c AW_GemAdd); the effect is not yet |
| Alchemy: strength | The alchemy factor is Alchemy plus 0.1 Intelligence plus 0.1 Luck. The strength x is the factor times the mortar and pestle's quality times fPotionStrengthMult (0.5). For each effect two ingredients share, the magnitude is x divided by fPotionT1MagMult (1.5), divided by the effect's base cost, and the duration is x divided by fPotionT1DurMult (0.5), divided by the base cost; an effect without magnitude or duration counts 1 for it. The other apparatus then adjust both. A harmful effect uses the alembic, a helpful one the retort, with the calcinator as the second apparatus. With both, the quality is 2 times the alembic's plus 3 times the calcinator's for a harmful effect, and for a helpful one 2 times the retort's plus the calcinator's when the effect has both magnitude and duration (otherwise two thirds of the sum plus 0.5); with one only, a harmful effect's quality is 1 plus the alembic's, a helpful one's the retort's (plus 0.5 unless it has both magnitude and duration); with only a calcinator, its own quality (plus 0.5 in the same case). A helpful effect, or any effect with only a calcinator, gets the quality added to magnitude and duration; a harmful effect with an alembic has them divided by it. Both are rounded; an effect that falls to 0 is lost. The potion's value is the strength times iAlchemyMod (2). | mwmechanics/alchemy.cpp updateEffects, applyTools, getAlchemyFactor | **not yet** |
| Alchemy: success | Each potion brewed fails when a roll 0 to 99 is above the alchemy factor; the ingredients are spent either way; a success trains Alchemy. Eating an ingredient reveals an effect when a roll 0 to 99 is not above (Alchemy plus 0.2 Intelligence plus 0.1 Luck) times the fatigue term; its duration is the roll divided by the smaller of that value and 100, times a quarter of the value. | mwmechanics/alchemy.cpp createSingle; spellutil.cpp rollIngredientEffect | **not yet** |
| Repair with a tool | The chance is (0.1 Strength plus 0.1 Luck plus Armorer) times the fatigue term. A roll 0 to 99 at or below it repairs the item by the larger of 1 and fRepairAmountMult (3) times the tool's quality times the roll, up to its maximum condition; the tool loses a use; Armorer is trained. | mwmechanics/repair.cpp | **not yet** |
| Repair at a merchant | Per item: the points missing divided by the larger of 1 and the item's maximum condition divided by its value (whole), times fRepairMult (1), at least 1, then the barter offer. | mwgui/merchantrepair.cpp | **in** (aw_dial.c AW_DialRepairPrice) |

| Breath and suffocation | Under water a being can hold its breath for fHoldBreathTime (20) seconds unless it has Water Breathing; then it takes fSuffocationDamage (3) health per second. | mwmechanics/actors.cpp updateDrowning | **not yet** (the engine's own water rule stands in) |

The distance beyond it, minus 1.5 times Acrobatics and the Jump magnitude (not below 0), is multiplied by fFallDistanceMult (0.07) and added to fFallDistanceBase (0); that is scaled by fFallAcroBase (0.25) plus fFallAcroMult (0.01) times (100 minus Acrobatics). | mwmechanics/character.cpp getFallDamage | **not yet** |

| Calculation | How it works | OpenMW 0.51 | AmiWind |

| Who sees a crime | Every being within fAlarmRadius (2,000) of the player that can report crimes counts. It saw the crime if it is the victim and was aware (an assault always is), or the crime is a murder (heard), or it has line of sight and the awareness check (below) passes. Theft and pickpocketing make a witness complain. | mwmechanics/mechanicsmanagerimp.cpp commitCrime | **partial**: theft is in (aw_crime.c AW_CrimeTheft: an owned item taken from the world, from an owned container or picked from an owned plant; the witnesses say their Thief line; `aw_placed crime` in the console shows the last theft and each witness); pickpocketing, trespass, assault and murder are not yet |
| Bounty and its side effects | A crime adds to the bounty: trespass iCrimeTresspass (5), pickpocket iCrimePickPocket (25), assault iCrimeAttack (40), murder iCrimeKilling (1000), theft the value taken times fCrimeStealing (1), at least 1. Each also lowers witnesses' disposition (trespass iDispTresspass -20, pickpocket fDispPickPocketMod -25, an assault iDispAttackMod -50 and fDispAttacking -10 for the victim, murder iDispKilling -50, theft fDispStealing -0.5 times the value) and provokes them to fight by a set amount (iFightTrespass 25, iFightPickpocket 25, iFightAttacking 50, iFightAttack 100 for a victim, iFightKilling 50, theft fFightStealing 50 times the value). A witness that is not a guard with Alarm 100 adds the provocation plus the disposition bias ((50 minus the disposition change) times fFightDispMult 0.2) plus the distance bias (iFightDistanceBase 20 minus fFightDistanceMultiplier 0.005 times the distance), all times its Alarm divided by 100, to its Fight (never past 100), and attacks at 100. | mwmechanics/mechanicsmanagerimp.cpp reportCrime | **partial**: theft is in (aw_crime_rules.c AW_CrimeTheftBounty, AW_CrimeTheftFight; the bounty is saved with the player's standing, aw_pcstanding.c); the disposition change of the witnesses and the other crimes are not yet |
| Guards and arrest | A guard that sees the player (line of sight and awareness) with a bounty of at least iCrimeThreshold (1,000) pursues; at iCrimeThreshold times iCrimeThresholdMultiplier (10), that is 10,000, it attacks. The dialogue offers a full payment of the bounty, a reduced payment of fCrimeGoldDiscountMult (0.5) of it and a turn-in payment of fCrimeGoldTurnInMult (0.9) of it. | mwmechanics/actors.cpp; mwworld/worldimp.cpp updateDialogueGlobals | **not yet** |
| Jail time | Going to jail clears the bounty and takes stolen goods back; the term is the bounty divided by iDaysinPrisonMod (100) in days, at least 1. | mwworld/worldimp.cpp goToJail | **not yet** |
| Awareness (being noticed) | The sneaker's term: when sneaking, fSneakSkillMult (1) times Sneak plus 0.2 Agility plus 0.1 Luck plus the weight of worn boots times fSneakBootMult (-1); not sneaking it is 0. | Lockpicking | The chance is (0.2 Agility plus 0.1 Luck plus Security) times the pick's quality times the fatigue term, plus fPickLockMult (-1) times the lock's level. | Disarming a trap | The chance is (0.2 Agility plus 0.1 Luck plus Security plus fTrapCostMult (0 in the data) times the trap spell's cost) times the probe's quality times the fatigue term; a roll 0 to 99 at or below it disarms; the probe loses a use. | mwmechanics/security.cpp probeTrap | **not yet** |
