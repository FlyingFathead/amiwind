## Short answer

Morrowind has no separate collision files. The collision of a model is stored inside the model's
own NIF (`Morrowind.bsa`, `Tribunal.bsa`, `Bloodmoon.bsa`, or a loose file under
`Data Files/Meshes/` that overrides the archive copy):

- a `RootCollisionNode` that is a direct child of the NIF's root node holds the authored
  collision triangles (the "collision mesh"); everything under it is invisible in game;
- without one, the engine builds the collision from all of the model's triangles
  ("autogenerated");
- a string marker on the root node (`NiStringExtraData`) can switch collision off (`NC...`),
  restrict it to the camera (`NCC`) or hide editor-only shapes (`MRK`);
- `AvoidNode` subtrees are an NPC pathfinding "avoid" shape, not player collision.

In the base game 2,680 of 5,798 NIFs (46 %) carry a `RootCollisionNode`; for the exterior
architecture and interior kit pieces it is the rule (79 % of `x/`, 84 % of `i/`). The authored
collision of every stair model measured is a ramp of at most 45 degrees, not the visible steps.

## The rules (OpenMW 0.51 as reference)

OpenMW reimplements Morrowind's physics on Bullet. Its loader,
`components/nifbullet/bulletnifloader.cpp` (class `NifBullet::BulletNifLoader`), states the
rules exactly. For a Morrowind NIF (version 4.0.0.2, older than 10.0.1.0):

| Rule | Where in `bulletnifloader.cpp` |
| --- | --- |
| The first `RootCollisionNode` among the **direct children of a root node** makes the mesh "authored": only geometry under `RootCollisionNode`s collides. | `handleRoot` (`colNode`), `handleNode` (`mIsCollisionNode`) |
| No `RootCollisionNode` at the root: `mAutogenerated`, every `NiTriShape`/`NiTriStrips` of the file collides. There is no hidden-flag test, so hidden shapes collide too. | `handleRoot`, `handleNode` |
| A `RootCollisionNode` deeper in an autogenerated mesh is treated as a plain `NiNode` (its triangles are added; a log line says so). | `handleNode` |
| An **empty** `RootCollisionNode` at the root: autogenerated, camera-only collision (as `NCC`). | `handleRoot` |
| Root `NiStringExtraData` `"MRK"`: shapes whose name starts with `Tri EditorMarker` are skipped. | `handleRoot` (`mHasTriMarkers`), `handleGeometry` |
| Root `NiStringExtraData` starting with `NC` (case-insensitive): `VisualCollisionType::Default`, i.e. no actor collision (`CollisionType_VisualOnly`). If the third letter is an upper-case `C` (`NCC`): `VisualCollisionType::Camera`, camera-only collision. | `handleRoot`; applied in `apps/openmw/mwphysics/physicssystem.cpp` `PhysicsSystem::addObject` |
| Geometry under an `AvoidNode` goes to `mAvoidCollisionShape` (NPC path avoidance), never to the actor collision. | `handleNode`, `handleGeometry` |
| `NiCollisionSwitch` with collision inactive: subtree skipped. `NiSwitchNode`: first child only. | `handleNode` |
| A node named `Bounding Box` gives the actor collision box of creatures and NPCs. | `findBoundingBox` |
| Files whose name starts with `x` and nodes with keyframe/path/roll controllers have animated collision. | `load` (`pathFileNameStartsWithX`), `handleNode` |
| `BSXFlags` (bit 2 collision, bit 32 editor markers) exist only for NIF >= 10.0.1.0 (later games), not for Morrowind. | `handleRoot` |

Shapes are triangle meshes (`Nif::NiTriShape::getCollisionShape` in `components/nif/node.cpp`
builds a `btTriangleMesh`; `Resource::TriangleMeshShape`), not convex hulls. Movement
(`components/misc/constants.hpp`, `apps/openmw/mwphysics/constants.hpp`,
`apps/openmw/mwphysics/stepper.cpp`, `movementsolver.hpp`):

| Constant | Value | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `sStepSizeUp` | 34 | highest step the stepper climbs |
| `sMaxSlope` | 46 degrees | steepest standable slope (`normal.z > cos 46`) |
| `sStepSizeDown` | 62 | stick-to-ground distance going down |
| `sMinStep`, `sMinStep2` | 10, 20 | extra stepping tries "to work around bad morrowind assets" |

AmiWind's engine works at 0.25 scale: `STEPSIZE` 8.5 (= 34 x 0.25) and `AW_WALKABLE_Z` 0.69
(46.4 degrees) in `engine/aga/src`, mirrored in `tools/player_hull.py`.

Findings:

- Morrowind's artists clip stairs with ramps. The collision of a staircase is a ramp at or
  under 45 degrees, just inside `sMaxSlope` 46, even where the visible stair is far steeper
  (Hlaalu interior stairs: 32-unit risers, 59-68 degrees). In Morrowind the player walks up the
  ramp, not the steps.
- The visible risers are all under the 34-unit step (32.0 is the largest regular riser). The
  visible stairs could be climbed step by step, but only on exact step surfaces. A convex hull
  over them becomes a 59-68 degree ramp that cannot be climbed.
- AmiWind's 0.69 limit (46.4 degrees) leaves about 1.4 degrees of margin over the 45-degree
  ramps. Quake's 0.7 (45.6 degrees) would also pass them, but nothing steeper.
- The exceptions are the 46-48 and 55-degree faces of `ex_hlaalu_dsteps_01` and `_03`
  (also not standable in OpenMW) and the two models whose RCN has real steps
  (`ex_vivec_b_wb_01`, `in_v_l_int_stairs_03`).

## How the AmiWind converter uses collision today

Since v0.0.48-dev2 (decided for the project, 22 October 2026) one collision-source rule applies in
every mesh converter: towns, Seyda Neen, rooms, census, prison ship, world scenery, CHIM exteriors
and CHIM rooms. It is `tools/collision_source.py`, written from OpenMW's rules (above):

- the model's `RootCollisionNode` when it is a direct child of a root node: only the triangles
  under `RootCollisionNode`s collide (source `rcn`);
- otherwise the drawn triangles (source `visual`); a `RootCollisionNode` deeper in the tree does
  not count (in the base game only `in_dagoth_bridge00` and `xin_dagoth_bridge00`);
- a root string starting with `NC` (any case) or `NCC`: nonsolid for the player (source `none`;
  AmiWind has no camera collision); an empty root `RootCollisionNode` too;
- `MRK`: the `Tri EditorMarker*` shapes never collide (`mwad.markers`, in `model_geometry`);
- `AvoidNode` subtrees never collide.

`export_refs` applies it to every model: a converter profile no longer opts in, and the
`meshes/i/`-only limit of the room converters (`interior_visual_profile`, `prepare_census`) no
longer limits the source. A profile may still require the authored collision
(`collision_source` `root_node`: the census and prison ship kit pieces,
`ex_imp_wall_stairs_01`/`_02`); a model without a root `RootCollisionNode` is then an export error.
Each export's `scenery-report.json` lists the source of every model (`collision_sources`: `rcn`,
`visual` or `none`, with the markers that decided it). `--collision-source profile` (on
`tools/build.py` and `tools/chim_build.py`, `AMIWIND_COLLISION_SOURCE`) keeps the rule before for
A/B: the profile opts in, only the exact strings `NC` and `NCO` make a model nonsolid.

Measured on the base game's 5,798 `Morrowind.bsa` NIFs (no game data written): the rule gives
2,680 `rcn`, 2,697 `visual` and 421 `none` (373 `NCO`, 48 `NCC`). Against the rule before, the
48 `NCC` models (tapestries, `furn_rug_big_*`, the `furn_c_t_*` sign plaques) become nonsolid and
the two Dagoth bridges collide with their drawn triangles; no root `RootCollisionNode` is empty;
the six `AvoidNode` shapes (one hidden shape in each `in_lava_*` pool, models with a
`RootCollisionNode`) never collide. Before the rule, the mesh converters already made `NCO` models nonsolid
(`source_collision`); `NCC` was solid.

The rest of the path:

- `tools/prepare_scenery.py` `model_geometry(..., collision=True)` reads every `NiTriShape`
  under any `RootCollisionNode` (or a node named `RootCollisionNode`) anywhere in the tree,
  hidden or not; `collision_source.model_collision` calls it only for a model with a root
  `RootCollisionNode`. The visual export (`collision=False`) skips hidden shapes and everything
  under a `RootCollisionNode`.
- `tools/prepare_mesh_bsp.py` `_prepare_model` passes that mesh to
  `tools/mesh_geometry.py` `collision_pieces`, shared by every converter and the estimators:
  - convex parts (`collision_parts`, at most 14 support points per hull,
    [COLLISION-CONVEX-LOSS-32](bugs/COLLISION-CONVEX-LOSS-32.md)). Those 14 points can leave the
    source surface outside the hull: a walkway deck that runs level and then slopes was cut as a chord
    up to 19.4 units under its planks, and the player sank into it
    ([CHIM-VIVEC-DOCK-WALKWAY-43](bugs/CHIM-VIVEC-DOCK-WALKWAY-43.md)). CHIM models of the 484 meshes
    measured in `config/collision-hull-surface.json` hold their walkable surface: a piece whose reduced hull
    leaves a point of its walkable faces more than 0.5 units deep gets points of those faces added until
    none is (`mesh_geometry.surface_hull`, since v0.0.46); walkable faces the stair rules then leave more than
    a step deep become authored plates. Keeping every point of such a piece, as first shipped, grew the
    memory of 1,196 of 1,351 frames and put six over the CHIM zone
    ([CHIM-HULL-SURFACE-HEAP-46](bugs/CHIM-HULL-SURFACE-HEAP-46.md)). `AMIWIND_COLLISION_HULL_POINTS`:
    `table`, the default; `all`, every mesh; `extremes`, the method before; `table-whole`, every point of
    such a piece (as first shipped). The collision surface gate of the CHIM model builder fails a mesh whose
    walkable collision lies more than a step over its pieces;
  - thin plates per authored surface for `hollow_collision`;
  - plates when the convex fill closes more than a step (`surface_collision_beyond`,
    exterior `x/` models of converted towns);
  - CHIM models: authored collision kept under a slab a walkway runs under for a list of mesh names
    (`ex_velothi_temple_01`, `_02`; `--collision-underside names`, the default). General rules for every mesh
    are experimental (`rule`, `split`, `whole`, see [EXPERIMENTAL_FLAGS.md](EXPERIMENTAL_FLAGS.md) and
    [CHIM-VOS-TEMPLE-WALKWAY-41](bugs/CHIM-VOS-TEMPLE-WALKWAY-41.md)).
- Editor markers (`MRK`, since v0.0.43-dev4): the shared NIF reader `model_geometry` drops the
  `Tri EditorMarker*` shapes of a root-`MRK` model from drawing and collision, and
  `tools/editor_markers.py` takes every marker placement (hidden markers, MRK models with nothing
  else to draw) out of the drawn scenery at the input, keeping it as an invisible sound, effect
  or collision object in the build's emitter table (`emitters.json`). The rules are in
  `src/mwad/markers.py`; [COLLISION-NC-FLAGS-32](bugs/COLLISION-NC-FLAGS-32.md).
- No converter reads `Bounding Box` nodes (the actor boxes come from the creature and NPC
  converters). The flora sprite path (`tools/prepare_tree_sprites.py` `collision_metadata`) keeps
  its own ground-vegetation rule. AmiWind has no camera collision.
- Collision is static; animated (`x`-prefixed) collision is not modelled.

- [COLLISION-NC-FLAGS-32](bugs/COLLISION-NC-FLAGS-32.md): `NCC` models were solid (the 667
  tapestry, rug and plaque placements of the Balmora rooms, 74 in the Seyda rooms); `AvoidNode`
  and empty `RootCollisionNode`s were not handled.

## Stair collision method 1 (the default)

Stair collision method 1 (`--stair-collision-method 1`, CHIM-STAIR-COLLISION-MESH-47) is the default since
v0.0.48-dev2 (decided for the project, 22 October 2026); `--stair-collision-method 0` keeps the method before (the
convex pieces with the stair rules) for A/B. It builds a flight's collision from the movement rules instead of
repairing convex pieces after the fact, and proves it by arithmetic instead of a walk (`tools/stair_mesh.py`):

- flights are found from the drawn level faces: treads that meet in plan within 2.5 units (a bevelled nosing between
  them) at a rise of 1 unit to a step (8.5 units: Morrowind's 34), three or more levels in a chain; a mesh with a
  RootCollisionNode takes a drawn face as a tread only where that node holds something under it (the original
  collides with it alone: a tree's roots are no flight; CHIM-STAIR-METHOD1-UNFIXED-48);
- the flights, their stairway mesh and the check come from the mesh as drawn, before a visual recipe or detail
  budget reduces it (the build's stair check reads it so); method 0's visible-tread rule keeps the reduced mesh;
- a flight whose collision (its RootCollisionNode, else the drawn mesh, as the convex pieces make it) already passes
  the check keeps it;
- otherwise it gets one ramp (the hull of its tread corners, the top through the nosings), or one solid block per
  tread where that ramp would stand more than a step over a tread, be steeper than walkable, or cut through other
  drawn geometry; the drawn steps are unchanged; the rest of the mesh is converted as before;
- the check: over every drawn tread with standing room, the surface the standing box rests on lies within a step
  of the tread, is walkable (normal z at least 0.69), leaves the box's height free, and neighbouring points whose
  drawn treads are within a step stand within a step. A mesh whose flights do not all pass keeps today's collision.

The check places the standing box's footprint over each tread point: the box rests on the highest support in
reach; a place where it does not fit or rests on a surface too steep to stand on is not a fault there, a tread with
no place to stand is. It was calibrated against the stair walks the v0.0.46 release build recorded, without walking
(`tools/stair_mesh_calibrate.py`): on the 2,896 tread points those walks covered (8.6 % of all), it agrees on 2,846
(98.3 %). Flights are climbed by one ramp per run of steps between landings where a ramp holds, by one block per
tread elsewhere (`stair_ramps` 'flight' keeps one ramp per whole flight selectable).

Walkways (rope bridges, docks, boards: rises of at most 2.5 units) are not stairs: they take in the level boards
they continue into and get sloped plates along their boards. The check also follows the standing box across every
seam between treads (from a ramp onto a landing or block, or the other way) and fails a lip over a step, a gap or a
blocked way.

Measured on the v0.0.46 release build's 1,380 exterior meshes (no build, no walk): 97 have flights (132); today's
collision fails the check on 34 of them; the check agrees with the recorded walks on 97.9 % of the 3,098 tread
points they covered. Method 1 changes 14 meshes (all their flights then pass; 13 failed before) at +11 % collision
bytes on those meshes (+3 % without one rope bridge and one curved stair), keeps 72 as they are, and cannot yet fix
11 (the ships, the shipwreck, the silt strider, an ashlander tent, Daedric ruins among them). Not tried in game yet.
`tools/stair_mesh_survey.py RUN --out DIR` repeats the measurement. Repeated on the v0.0.47 release build for the
default switch (22 October 2026, read only): the same 1,380 meshes, 97 with 132 flights, and the same result (14
changed, 72 kept, 11 not fixed). The new collision-source rule changes nothing there: CHIM models already read their
RootCollisionNode, and none of the 48 `NCC` models has a flight.

How a default build applies it:

- **Per mesh.** The CHIM model builder counts each distinct mesh's flights as drawn (`stair_mesh.flight_unit`,
  unit kind `stair-flights`, cached by content). Only the meshes with a flight are converted by method 1's own
  worker and unit kind (`mesh-stair`). Every other mesh keeps the default worker, profile and unit key (method 1 is
  method 0 exactly on a mesh without a flight), so the switch rebuilds the flight meshes only.
- **The check.** The check of every flight mesh goes into `chim-world/chim-stair-check.json` and the build summary.
  The build fails on a mesh that fails it under the collision it ships with, unless the mesh is on
  `config/stair-check-known.json`: the 11 meshes method 1 cannot fix yet (the ship, the shipwreck, the silt
  strider, an ashlander tent, two skewed Daedric ruins and a ruin entry, a shack, a tall house, a Hlaalu balcony and
  `ex_hlaalu_dsteps_01`), one class, CHIM-STAIR-METHOD1-UNFIXED-48. They are reported (status `known`), never
  dropped.
- **The authored stair ramp stays a ramp** (`tools/authored_ramps.py`, `stair_mesh.keep_stair_ramps`). A ramp of
  the collision mesh is a walkable face (normal z at least 0.69) more than 10 degrees from level. It belongs to a
  staircase when its centre lies over a drawn flight. The check (plane arithmetic): just over every such ramp, the
  collision must not be a face steeper than walkable, nor stand more than a step over it, where the authored
  surfaces themselves are not. A convex piece that does either becomes the authored plates of its own triangles
  (exact standing bevels), as the stair-plate rules do for a piece that invents a steep face. The plates are kept
  only when the stair check is not worse with them. Measured on the v0.0.47 build: 74 CHIM meshes have stair ramps
  in their RootCollisionNode, method 0 covers or steepens them on 14, and the guard repairs 12 (`ex_de_ship` is on
  the known list; `ex_v_palace_steps_01` keeps its pieces because the plates failed its stair check). In the rooms
  and the Seyda Neen scenery, 33 models with a RootCollisionNode have stair ramps (the Hlaalu, Velothi, common and
  Vivec stair pieces among them). After the new source rule, none of the room stair pieces has a ramp covered or
  steepened. The three that do are exterior meshes in the Seyda Neen scenery, all on the known list or repaired on
  CHIM (`ex_de_ship`, `ex_de_shipwreck`, `ex_common_house_tall_02`). The room converters do not run method 1.
  Applied to every walkable slope rather than to stair ramps only, the same repair would grow those meshes'
  collision by 58 % (a rock by x120) for no staircase, so it stays on stair ramps.

## Cost of using the authored collision

Clip-node cost is estimated with the converter's own estimator (`collision_pieces` plus
`prepare_mesh_bsp.standing_planes`, as in `tools/world_estimate_data.py` `mesh_costs`), summed
over placements. The compiled clip hull shares and splits planes, so these are relative numbers.
The compiled collision budget per map is 65,520 nodes (`prepare_mesh_bsp`).

| Change | Area | Clip nodes before | after |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| RCN as the source instead of the visual mesh (same proxy rules) | Seyda Neen exterior (86 models) | 63,576 | 13,247 (-79 %) |
| | Balmora rooms (all 166 models that have an RCN) | 241,216 | 111,108 (-54 %) |
| | Seyda rooms (all 120 models that have an RCN) | 132,555 | 98,707 (-26 %) |
| Exact plates (hollow + exact bevels) for every model instead of the current proxies | Balmora exterior | 288,146 | 2,213,931 (x7.7) |
| | Seyda Neen exterior | 35,781 | 933,771 (x26) |
| | Vivec Arena exterior | 142,511 | 532,853 (x3.7) |
| | Balmora rooms | 569,887 | 3,909,530 (x6.9) |
| | Seyda rooms | 169,059 | 1,345,814 (x8.0) |

With plates everywhere, single rooms reach 160,000-530,000 estimated nodes (Addamasartus 529,119;
Fighters Guild 196,200). That is far over the per-map budget, so "exact plates for everything" is
not affordable on the legacy engine. Using the RCN as the source is cheaper than today everywhere
it applies.

Measured again for the default switch (v0.0.48-dev2), on the v0.0.47 release build's own exports,
before (the rule before: profile opt-in, `NCC` solid) and after (the collision-source rule), same
proxy rules and profiles as each converter passes, summed over placements:

| Area | Placements | Changed | Clip nodes before | after |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Balmora rooms (43) | 3,408 | 1,805 | 507,527 | 285,836 (-44 %) |
| Seyda Neen rooms (13 and Addamasartus) | 770 | 378 | 149,774 | 114,304 (-24 %) |
| Vivec rooms | 13,271 | 6,220 | 4,302,819 | 3,569,324 (-17 %) |
| Seyda Neen scenery | 2,789 | 1,568 | 344,060 | 93,495 (-73 %) |

These differ from the 8 October table above because the baseline differs: there every model with
an RCN was compared against its visual mesh; here today's converters (rooms already used the RCN
for `meshes/i/`, and `NCO` was already nonsolid) against the rule. On the changed placements only:
Balmora rooms 234,412 to 12,721 (-95 %), Seyda Neen scenery 329,641 to 79,076 (-76 %), in line with
the earlier -54 % and -79 %. The CHIM exteriors do not change (they already read every RCN).

## Recommendations

Status 22 October 2026: 1, 2 (for CHIM stairs, through stair collision method 1 and the
authored-ramp rule) and 5 are in source for v0.0.48-dev2; 3 and 4 stand.

1. **One collision-source rule for every converter.** Every mesh converter (towns, Seyda Neen,
   rooms, census, prison ship, world scenery, CHIM) should follow Morrowind:
   - Use the root `RootCollisionNode` when present, otherwise the visual triangles. Make this
     the default of `export_refs`, not a per-profile opt-in, and remove the `meshes/i/`-only
     condition in `interior_visual_profile` and `prepare_census`.
   - `NC*` means nonsolid (`collision_none`). `NCC` also means nonsolid for the player, because
     there is no camera collision.
   - `MRK` drops `Tri EditorMarker*` shapes; `AvoidNode` subtrees are excluded.
   - An empty root `RootCollisionNode` means nonsolid (none exist in the base game).

   Put this in one shared function next to `model_geometry` with a unit test on synthetic NIFs.
   This repairs COLLISION-RCN-SCOPE-32 and COLLISION-NC-FLAGS-32 and lowers clip nodes where it
   changes anything.
2. **Stairs: collide with the authored collision first.** With the RCN as the source, the
   convex proxy of a stair's ramp RCN is the ramp itself (26-45 degrees), so it is walkable
   without a stair heuristic. The stair rule (exact plates where a convex piece over a staircase
   is steeper than walkable, clip ramp as fallback) is still needed for:
   - models without an RCN;
   - RCNs with real steps (`ex_vivec_b_wb_01`, `in_v_l_int_stairs_03`);
   - convex pieces that merge a ramp with neighbouring surfaces.

   Run it on the RCN, not the visual mesh.
3. **Keep the walkable limit at or above 0.69**, and never round authored 45-degree ramp normals
   down: the margin is about 1.4 degrees.
4. **No plates for everything** on the legacy engine (estimate x3.7-x26 clip nodes, rooms over
   the budget). Plates stay limited to the hand-made list, stair pieces and closed-space
   exceptions. CHIM's per-chunk collision can revisit this with real triangle collision.
5. **Gate it.** A converter report should list, per model, the collision source (RCN, visual,
   none) and the NC/NCC/MRK markers. A test should fail when a model with a root
   `RootCollisionNode` is converted from its visual mesh, or when an NC model is solid.

## How the numbers were measured

Read-only, in the builder container (`amiwind-builder`, `--network none`, `--pull never`), with
the Data Files mounted read-only. Steps:

1. Every NIF in the three archives and the loose `Meshes` folder was parsed with the repository's
   PyFFI adapter (`prepare_scenery.nif_reader`), then walked with OpenMW's rules (above) and
   with AmiWind's (`model_geometry`).
2. Town placements are the base-master exterior references inside each town frame
   (`config/balmora.json`, `seyda_area.json`, `vivec_arena.json`; 256-unit margin). Room
   placements are `mwad.interior.select_geometry` of each converted cell.
3. Stair figures come from the triangles that `model_geometry` exports. Treads have a normal
   within 10 degrees of vertical; levels are clustered at 1 unit, steps are consecutive levels
   2-34 apart, and pitch is rise over the horizontal distance between the first and last tread
   centroids.
4. "Steepest face over a tread" is the lowest proxy face above a tread centroid among the
   proxies that start at the tread.
