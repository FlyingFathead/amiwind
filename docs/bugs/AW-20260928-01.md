# AW-20260928-01: Prison ship to deck transition is intermittently very slow or freezes (FS-UAE)

## Status: 8 October 2026

Open; tagged performance. Cause unknown. First reported 28 September 2026 (v0.0.19 work) as a
freeze at the ship hatch; reported again by the owner on 8 October 2026 (v0.0.32-dev1 and dev2) as
"scene clearing from prison ship to the deck; sometimes on FS-UAE seems absolutely sluggish".
Report page written 8 October 2026; the earlier record is in the
[frozen notes](../journals/BUGS-NOTES-v0.0.29.md#aw-20260928-01-potential-prison-ship-exit-freeze-during-intro).

## Symptom

Leaving the prison ship interior for the deck (the opening sequence) sometimes takes very long in
FS-UAE; on 28 September 2026 the first attempt froze at the hatch ("Seyda Neen / E: Enter") and a
second attempt worked. Seen on the owner's Linux machine: FS-UAE 3.1.66, Ubuntu 24.04.

## Where

The scene change from the prison ship interior to the deck/exterior map (`engine/aga/src/aw_scene.c`
scene load, map loading, the opening music). Related: AW-20260928-07 (slow opening exterior,
excessive residency), [SEYDA-READ-SLOW-31](SEYDA-READ-SLOW-31.md) (read patterns of Seyda Neen
loads), [MUSIC-OPENING-CLIP-31](MUSIC-OPENING-CLIP-31.md) and [AUDIO-03](AUDIO-03.md) (opening
music and the post-movie load).

## How it happened

Unknown. Candidates:

- a cold host disk cache on the first run (the emulator waits for every block the host reads,
  [BENCH-HOST-STORAGE-32](BENCH-HOST-STORAGE-32.md));
- the size and heap of the deck/exterior map;
- the order of music and map loading;
- read patterns like those of SEYDA-READ-SLOW-31.

## Why it was not caught

The crossing benchmarks cover Seyda Neen sub-cells, not the ship-to-deck transition, and runs are
usually warm (host cache filled by earlier runs).

## Reproduction

New game, the opening sequence, leave the hold for the deck; read the "Scene ready: <map>, <ms>,
<hunk bytes>, arrival ..." console line. Compare a cold run (host cache dropped or first start
after boot) with a warm one.
