## Symptom
In the v0.0.43-dev2 build (2026_10_18_v0.0.43-dev2_full-batch3_f29d984) the 8 cells around Balmora's centre cell,
(-4,-3) to (-2,-1), are listed for CHIM and lie inside Balmora's frame, yet the exterior checklist keeps each of them
as a classic cell: a 96-unit strip (384 original units) along the frame's edge stays on the classic open world. The
build ships 15 legacy maps for those strips (vf0699 to vf0864, 0.49 to 1.39 MB each, 11.4 MB together); being cut
from whole cells, they carry 286 to 829 placements each. Between Balmora and the CHIM cells around it the player
crosses that classic strip.

## Where
`config/balmora.json` had no `handoff_core`, so `tools/town_config.py` `handoff_core` used the inset method of
before v0.0.36: the frame's bounds inset by 96 units on every side. `tools/exterior_plan.py` `town_cores` takes the
handoff core as the town's share of the map, and everything outside it is classic.

## How it happened
Seyda Neen (v0.0.36) and Vivec (v0.0.36; whole cells since v0.0.40) were given cores on the cell grid
(`owned_cells`, an explicit `handoff_core`, a terrain margin past the edge). Balmora kept the inset because it was an older table row. While its neighbours were
classic the strip did not show; since the regional CHIMport put every cell around Balmora on CHIM, the strip is the
only classic ground left there.

## Why it was not caught
The cells are in the CHIM cell list, so every count of the list called them CHIM; only the build's exterior
checklist names the strips (format `bsp`, `town_owners` balmora).

## Reproduction
Any full build with Balmora on CHIM: `exterior-checklist.json` lists (-4,-3), (-4,-2), (-4,-1), (-3,-3), (-3,-1),
(-2,-3), (-2,-2) and (-2,-1) as `bsp` with a `bsp_core` strip 96 units wide.

## Repair
Balmora owns its 3 x 3 cells like Seyda Neen: `owned_cells` (-4,-3) to (-1,0) (half-open), `handoff_core` equal to
the bounds (the cell grid), and `terrain_margin` 768 (ground only, no placements: at least draw distance + 32 past the
handoff line, CHIM-FAR-BAND-GAP-36). Source cell, radius, centre, scale, bounds, sub-cell cores and the placements
are unchanged. The inset method stays selectable (`"handoff_core": "inset"`, the Arena).

## Verification
CHIM stage alone on Balmora's frame (`tools/chim_build.py --area balmora --validate --stats --sdk`), A = the line
head f29d984, B = this change, 18 October 2026:

| | A (inset) | B (cell grid) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Placements | 1,488 | 1,488 |
| Ring peak / headroom | 4,957,168 / 1,285,136 bytes | 4,957,168 / 1,285,136 bytes |
| Chunks | 576 | 900 (the terrain margin) |
| CHIM world bytes | 17,390,344 | 18,902,420 (+1.5 MB) |
| Validation, heap, zone walk, stair gate | passed | passed |
| Collision union fallbacks | none | none |

The 15 legacy strip maps (11.4 MB) leave the build: about 9.9 MB less in all. `tests/test_town_grid.py` checks that
every town of the default build owns whole cells and that the exterior checklist keeps no town-owned classic cell;
`tests/test_world_regions.py` and `tests/test_town_frame_residents.py` record Balmora's new core and owned cells.

## Prevention
A town core off the cell grid fails `tests/test_town_grid.py`.
