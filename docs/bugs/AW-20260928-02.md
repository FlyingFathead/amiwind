## Status: 10 October 2026

Open. Suspected to be the same mechanism as [HANG-IDLE-DUSK-35](HANG-IDLE-DUSK-35.md) and
[SEYDA-LOAD-HANG-30](SEYDA-LOAD-HANG-30.md): a music stream read that never returns while the main loop waits for it.
Not captured in a debugger, so not confirmed.

## Symptom

During a v0.0.19 playtest on FS-UAE (28 September 2026) the game froze at the dock and the menu after leaving the
prison ship, with the music looping. A third run was stable.

## Where

Unknown. The looping music points to the main loop stopping while the sound hardware replays its last buffer, which
is what [HANG-IDLE-DUSK-35](HANG-IDLE-DUSK-35.md) showed for the music refill read (`aw_music.c`).

## How it happened

Unknown. Recorded in the v0.0.29 notes as a potential dock/menu freeze with looping music.

## Why it was not caught

Seen once in a manual playtest; the route was never repeated in a soak run.

## Reproduction

Once: leave the prison ship, walk to the dock, open the menu (FS-UAE).

## Repair

Expected to be covered by the repair of [HANG-IDLE-DUSK-35](HANG-IDLE-DUSK-35.md) (the music stream no longer blocks
the main loop).

## Verification

To do: the ship-to-dock route repeated in the soak harness after that repair.

## Prevention

See [HANG-IDLE-DUSK-35](HANG-IDLE-DUSK-35.md).
