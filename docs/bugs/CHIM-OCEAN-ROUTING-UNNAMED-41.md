_Last updated with AmiWind v0.0.42-dev1 (17 October 2026)._

## Symptom
The second try of the first regional build stopped in world terrain with "Ocean routing cells must be selected
and have no existing core owner", naming no cell and not saying which of the two rules failed.

## Where
`tools/prepare_world_regions.py`, `prepare`, the check of the cells taken as open ocean.

## How it happened
One message covered two rules (an ocean cell not in the selection, or one that already has an open-world map of
its own) and listed nothing.

## Why it was not caught
The rule "every failure names its unit" was not checked for this message.

## Reproduction
A build whose selection takes a cell as ocean while an open-world map owns it.

## Repair
The message names every cell and which rule it broke. The build's cells were narrowed: the open sea west of the
classic seabed strip near (-12,1) to (-12,4) stays classic with it in v0.0.41 ([CHIMPORT-OCEAN-LAND-41](CHIMPORT-OCEAN-LAND-41.md)).

## Verification
The next regional build passes world terrain.

## Prevention
Every build-stage error names its unit, input and the check that failed.
