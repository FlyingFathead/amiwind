_Last updated with AmiWind v0.0.48-dev2 (23 October 2026)._

## Symptom

The v0.0.48-dev2 build (try 10) stopped in the image step's actor ground check: Bradil Elarven (reference 180984,
Vivec, St. Delyn Glassworker's Hall, room map `vdi010`) had no support under his feet ("Initial mesh contact outside
support tolerance", support status `unsupported`, feet at z 3832, the authored height). The support bake had left him
unfitted (`unsupported`).

## Where

`tools/actor_grounding.py`, the support bake's centre probe (`_Fitter._setup`).

## How it happened

The game data places him at x 4160, y 3776: on the 256-unit grid of the room's floor pieces, exactly on the shared
edge of two of them (`in_v_s_wall_column_01` and `in_v_s_int_corner_03`, both with their floor at 3824.2). A point
trace exactly on that edge passes between the two pieces and finds the next collision 26 units lower (40 in the CHIM
room), past the probe's 40-unit reach: the bake reported no support and left him at his authored height, 7.8 units over
the floor. His feet, a few units off the edge, stand on the floor pieces.

## Why it was not caught

The v0.0.47 room collision had no gap under that edge (he was grounded at z 3824.56); the room collision changed
with the collision defaults of 22 October 2026, and the actor ground check first reached the CHIM rooms in v0.0.48.

## Reproduction

Bake the Glassworker's Hall: `Actor support: vdi010 180984 unsupported`.

## Repair

`actor_grounding.centre_support`: when the centre probe finds nothing (`unsupported`), the centre is probed 1/16
unit to each side in x and y and the highest support is kept. A centre with support, or blocked, is used as before,
so every other placement is fitted exactly as before. On the try 10 room map he is now supported and `unchanged` at
z 3824.45, and all 23 contact samples pass on the CHIM room's own collision.

Checked by `tests/test_vivec_room_contacts.py` (seam, supported centre, no support, blocked centre). Not yet seen in a
build.
