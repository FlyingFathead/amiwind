## Status: 20 October 2026

Open. PERSISTING, NOT RESOLVED in a build: reported again with v0.0.46 by the maintainer: the image spans
more volumes than its payload needs, and no partition size leaves a planned headroom. Repaired in source on the development branch
v0.0.46-span-what-we-need (the layout planner below); it counts as fixed once a build made with it ships.

## Symptom

Nothing gates the number of world partitions or the growth of the image: v0.0.36-dev3 has three world partitions on two disks, like v0.0.35-dev1, and no check noticed.

v0.0.46 (release build of 20 October 2026, one HDF of 3,623,911,424 bytes): three partitions where two hold the
payload. DH0 1,879,048,192 bytes (payload 1,457,977,095 bytes, 386,626,560 free); DW0 134,217,728 bytes holding only
the 1,348 CHIM region frame maps (payload 1,141,356 bytes, 132,062,720 free: 98 % empty); DW1 1,610,612,736 bytes with
the CHIM world (payload 1,328,723,736 bytes, 239,373,312 free). Every partition passed the gate.

## Where

The disk-layout gate (`require_disk_layout` and the partition plan checks in tools/world_volumes.py).

## How it happened

The gate enforces the four Amiga limits only: each partition under 2 GiB, each start below 2 GiB, files well under 2 GiB, each drive image under 4 GiB. It sets no limit on the total size or the number of partitions, so the image can grow without a failing check.

The v0.0.46 DW0 came from two rules of the legacy layout. The open-world maps the region directory names were split
from the boot payload by halving the total payload (`world_volumes.balanced`), a rule made when the legacy region maps
were half of the game; on an all-CHIM build those maps are the 1.1 MB of frame maps, the boot payload alone is past
the halfway mark, so every map went to a volume of its own. The CHIM world was then added as one more volume after
it. And every partition was sized payload + 20 % + 16 MiB in 128 MiB steps, at least 128 MiB, so the 1.1 MB volume
became 128 MiB and the others carried 239 to 387 MB of free space. Nothing in the engine needs DW0: the engine adds
`AW_WORLD0:id1` ... `AW_WORLD<n-1>:id1` to its search path from `world/volumes.awv` (common.c) and opens a frame map
from whichever volume has it.

## Why it was not caught

The project target of one HDF (IMAGE-ONE-HDF-TARGET-36) had no check behind it.

## Reproduction

Read the gate: a build with three world partitions on two disks passes.

## Persisting since

Persisting since [WORLD-THIRD-PARTITION-32](WORLD-THIRD-PARTITION-32.md) (v0.0.32-dev1, 8 October 2026, open): the image grew a fourth partition and only the launchers were adapted. [AMIGA-DISK-2GIB-LIMIT-33](AMIGA-DISK-2GIB-LIMIT-33.md) (closed) gated the platform limits, not growth. See IMAGE-ONE-HDF-TARGET-36.

## Repair

20 October 2026, development branch v0.0.46-span-what-we-need: the layout planner (`tools/layout_plan.py`, the
compact layout, now the default; [build output](../BUILD_OUTPUT.md#disk-layout-the-fewest-partitions-each-with-headroom)).
The payload preflight plans the fewest partitions the payload needs before the image step writes anything: the
open-world maps go onto the CHIM world volume first, then onto DH0, and another partition opens only when neither has
room; each partition is sized from the FFS blocks of its files plus a fixed headroom (100 MiB by default, builder
setting `partition_headroom_mib`, a separate one for the boot partition possible), and the 2 GiB limits open a further
partition rather than shrink the headroom. The plan is written to the run (`image/layout-plan.json`); the image step
follows it and refuses when a partition's payload moved by more than its headroom. The partitions are written at the
same time (one worker each; the boot partition and the CHIM world volume took 42.0 s and 43.4 s one after the other
in the v0.0.46 release build). The disk-layout gate now also refuses a partition with less free space than its
headroom, a partition over 16 MiB that is mostly empty (free space over its headroom plus half its size) and a plan
with a partition of maps that would fit into another one. The legacy layout stays selectable (`--disk-layout legacy`).

## Verification

- `tests/test_layout_plan.py`: the release-shaped payload plans two partitions; maps fill the CHIM volume, then DH0,
  then a new volume; the headroom is never shrunk; payloads past the limit are refused with their sizes; the
  minimal-partitions, headroom and follow-the-plan refusals; the FFS size estimate equals what xdftool writes; the
  partitions written at once equal those written one by one (same bytes in the same blocks); the engine's search
  path finds the frame maps in both layouts; a failed write puts the maps back.
- On the v0.0.46 release build's staged files (a hard-linked copy; the frame maps read back from its DW0), the
  planner and the image step's partition and drive part: 2 partitions on one drive of 3,076,554,752 bytes (the
  release: 3 partitions, 3,623,911,424 bytes). DH0 1,598,029,824 bytes (payload 1,457,977,095, 105,678,336 free,
  6.6 %); DW0 1,478,492,160 bytes with the CHIM world and the 1,348 frame maps (payload 1,329,865,092, 105,166,336
  free, 7.1 %). The planned free space equalled the written free space to the byte. Both partitions written at once
  in 62.5 s on 4 cores (the release: 85.4 s one after the other on 24; not a like-for-like time: different core
  budgets and host load).
- That two-partition HDF with the image's own engine, engine boot smoke in FS-UAE (68040, JIT): PASS, Seyda Neen and
  Balmora loaded (three map changes) with the music on, off and on again; and PASS with the region frame map vf0773
  (on `AW_WORLD0`) loaded first, then Balmora.

## Prevention

The disk-layout gate refuses mostly-empty partitions and partitions the payload does not need, and the plan with
its per-partition payload, size and free space is printed by every build and recorded in `image/build.json`
(`disk_layout_plan`).
