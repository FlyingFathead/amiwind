_Last updated with AmiWind v0.0.57-dev4 (30 October 2026)._

## Status: 30 October 2026

Closed as a wrong diagnosis (30 October 2026): the yurt was never missing from the table. Its own reduction row
(POLYCOUNT-CAMPS-ALDRUHN-57) changed what the converter's visible-tread rule saw, so its collision changed; measured
with and without the row on the same staged mesh, only the row makes the gate fail. The row and the table entry
added here are both taken out: [RECIPE-ROW-MOVES-COLLISION-57](RECIPE-ROW-MOVES-COLLISION-57.md). The text below is
the first, wrong reading, kept as it was written.

## Symptom

The first build of the Urshilaku Camp MiniWind stopped at the collision surface gate: the walkable collision of
ex_ashl_tent_03 lay 64 units under its convex pieces, so a player would sink into the yurt and meet a step too high.

## Where

ex_ashl_tent_03, the large yurt of the Ashlander camps (Urshilaku, Zainab and Ahemmusa Camps).

## How it happened

`config/collision-hull-surface.json` lists the meshes whose convex pieces keep their source surface. The other three
yurts (ex_ashl_tent_01, 02 and 04) were in it; ex_ashl_tent_03 was not.

## Why it was not caught

No earlier build that runs the gate on its own frame placed this yurt.

## Reproduction

Always: any CHIM build whose frame holds ex_ashl_tent_03.

## Repair

ex_ashl_tent_03 added to the table (its count 413 to 414). Collision still comes from the original mesh.

## Verification

The Urshilaku Camp MiniWind built past the gate (run 2026_10_30_v0.0.57-dev1_miniwind-urshilaku-camp_100a49f).

## Prevention

The gate itself: it names the mesh and its depth, and stops the build.
