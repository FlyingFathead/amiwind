_Last updated with AmiWind v0.0.43-dev3 (18 October 2026)._

## Status: 10 October 2026

Open. Found by an independent read-only engine review; confirmed in the source. Part of the "engine C speed pass".

## Symptom

No visible symptom: `R_EmitEdge` computes `(int) ceil(v)` for both ends of every edge it emits, each a call into the
C library in double precision plus a float-to-int conversion
([ENGINE-FPCR-DANCE-36](ENGINE-FPCR-DANCE-36.md)).

## Where

`engine/aga/src/r_draw.c`, `R_EmitEdge`.

## How it happened

Quake's C reference code; the x86 build used assembly instead.

## Why it was not caught

The renderer's hot paths were not profiled per call on the 68040.

## Reproduction

Source reading (`ceil` in `R_EmitEdge`).

## Repair

Proposed (not done): an inline ceiling (convert with truncation, add one when the value was not whole and
positive), checked pixel-identical at the fixed cameras.

## Verification

Pending.

## Prevention

Pending.
