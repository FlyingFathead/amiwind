## Status: 9 October 2026

Open. The largest single cause (BUILD-CACHE-PER-WORKSPACE-33) is repaired in source; the stage
keys that rerun the CHIM stage and the engine on every build are tracked under their own IDs.

## Symptom

A MiniWind build (Balmora on CHIM with its interiors, quick test defaults: no movies, unreferenced
voices and NPCs left out) reused 15 of 25 stages from a v0.0.33-rc1 build and still estimated
about 104 minutes at 21 %.

## Where

MiniWind builds (`tools/build.py --miniwind`) with `--reuse-from`.

## How it happened

Measured from the build's state and logs (4 workers in total for the whole build):

| Stage | Wall | Why it ran |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| media | 1,195 s | the quick test exclusions change its command (expected); its per-file cache was empty on the new workspace: 4,862 of 4,875 sounds converted (BUILD-CACHE-PER-WORKSPACE-33), at 1 to 3 workers |
| intro | 292 s | the exclusions change its command (expected) |
| census | 189 s | depends on intro |
| engine | 91 s | never reused: the SDK is not in its key (BUILD-ENGINE-KEY-SDK-33) |
| chim, balmora, hand-catalog | still running | chim's key holds the version and the release file list (BUILD-KEY-OVERBROAD-33) |

The quick test exclusions themselves apply before the work: 4,875 sounds were imported (4,158
referenced voices and 717 effects) out of 7,164; the 2,289 others were never read.

## Why it was not caught

MiniWind timings were measured in one workspace with warm caches, on a full worker budget.

## Reproduction

A MiniWind build on a new workspace with `--reuse-from` a full build in another workspace and a
small `--jobs` budget.

## Repair

In progress: per-file caches read the reuse source's workspace (BUILD-CACHE-PER-WORKSPACE-33, in
source); stage keys narrowed to what each stage reads (BUILD-KEY-OVERBROAD-33,
BUILD-ENGINE-KEY-SDK-33).

## Verification

Planned: the same MiniWind configuration rebuilt on a new workspace with the repairs, stage by stage
against this build.

## Prevention

The builder profile (docs/performance/BUILDER_PROFILE.md) lists reruns despite `--reuse-from` and
stages converting files that another workspace holds.
