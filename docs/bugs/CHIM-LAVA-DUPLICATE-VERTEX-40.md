15 October 2026, v0.0.41 candidate: 50 focused lava/polygon/terrain checks
and 47 emulator preset/launcher checks pass. Both original failing chunks pass
coverage against the unchanged cached geometry. Full combined-world, exact
source suite, native, package and shipment checks remain pending.

## Symptom

The v0.0.40 combined-world build stopped on two chunks in cell (2, -2).
Readback of the exact cached units found five affected coverage samples:
each has one lava surface and lava contents (-5) at the required depth.
A tiny edge collapses to identical adjacent vertices when the chunk is
translated into float32 frame coordinates. The polygon test mistook this
zero-length edge for a boundary through every sample.

The v0.0.41 candidate skips only exactly identical adjacent XY vertices.
It preserves edge ambiguity, outside rejection and empty/degenerate rejection;
no geometry, collision, pool height, tolerance or engine format is changed.
Full-world validation, native checks, fresh package readback and delivery
remain separate and pending. No coverage count is promoted.

## Where

CHIM terrain coverage in original exterior cell (2, -2), chunks (7,13)
and (7,14), after float32 frame translation.

## How it happened

The convex-polygon checker returned boundary ambiguity for the cross product
of a zero-length edge, regardless of the actual sample position.

## Why it was not caught

Existing lava fixtures exercised terrain intersections and overlapping pools,
but did not collapse an adjacent vertex pair during float32 translation.
The larger source apron exposed these pool corners in the combined build.

## Reproduction

Translate a clipped quadrilateral with a very short edge to a frame whose
coordinate spacing makes the edge's two endpoints identical in float32.
A point strictly inside the resulting triangle was rejected as an edge.

## Repair

Skip exactly identical adjacent XY endpoints when testing polygon containment.
Keep the existing epsilon for genuine edges and reject empty/degenerate input.
AmiQuake uses float32 BSP vertices and point-hull contents; those representations
are retained. Only the offline containment check changes.

## Verification

15 October 2026: exact corrected source passed 4,225 tests: 4,219 passed,
three Windows skips and three tracked Census expected failures. The Amiga
executable compiled. The full world then stopped at the separate tower-window
support issue; full-world/native/package/shipment acceptance remains pending.

Exact cached terrain readback confirms all five samples have one surface and
lava contents at height 124, beneath the pool surface at height 128. The earlier
source suite ran 4,222 tests: 4,216 passed, three Windows skips and three tracked
Census expected failures. That suite does not certify the v0.0.41 correction.
Focused regressions and exact cached-chunk checks pass; full successor checks remain pending.

## Prevention

Regression cases cover float32-collapsed adjacent corners in both windings,
repeated closing vertices, true boundary ambiguity, outside and degenerate input.
Native visual confirmation remains pending the complete fresh image.
