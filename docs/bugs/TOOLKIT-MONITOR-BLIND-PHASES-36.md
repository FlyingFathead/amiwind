## Status: 11 October 2026

Open. Reported by the owner while watching the v0.0.36-dev3 image step.

## Symptom

During long build phases that print nothing (the CHIM statistics, the image step's map writing), the Monitor's log view stops
and nothing else on the page shows that the build is working, what it is doing, or how long it has been silent.

## Where

The Toolkit Monitor tab (`amiwind-toolkit/monitor.html`) and its read-only server endpoints.

## How it happened

The first Monitor shows the stage logs, progress and stages only; silent phases leave it with nothing to show.

## Why it was not caught

The Monitor was tested on phases that print lines.

## Reproduction

Always: open the Monitor during a silent phase (for example the image step writing CHIM maps).

## Repair

An Activity panel fed by the stage's live read/write trace (`profile/reads/<stage>.txt`): files written and read in the last
seconds, the tool and function currently running, the time since the last log line, cores in use; the phases inside each
stage and a live stage timeline under the log. Together with BUILD-SILENT-PHASES-36, which makes the builder itself report.

## Verification

To do: during a silent phase the Monitor shows live activity and the silence time.

## Prevention

Every build phase is visible in the Monitor, from the builder's own lines or its trace.
