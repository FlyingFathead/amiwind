_Last updated with AmiWind v0.0.57-dev1 (30 October 2026)._

## Status: 30 October 2026

Fixed in the source on the v0.0.57 alive branch; in-game check pending.

## Symptom

Playing v0.0.56, the maintainer found the creatures placed but standing completely still: mud crabs do nothing,
cliff racers do not fly, and hostile creatures do not attack when the player comes near.

## Where

Every cell with a levelled creature reference: 6,352 references in `Morrowind.esm`, 5,082 of them outdoors (measured
on the maintainer's master, 30 October 2026). The ambient table builder (`tools/ambient_lut.py`) and the engine's
ambient module (`engine/aga/src/aw_ambient.c`).

## Cause

The ambient table (`id1/npc-ambient.lut`) held one row per placed NPC or creature whose first package is Wander,
keyed by the reference number and the actor's id. A levelled reference names a list, not a creature, so it got no
row; when its cell joins, the engine rolls the list and spawns a creature under the reference number with the
creature's id, and the lookup found nothing. Most wild creatures come from levelled lists, so they stood. In OpenMW
0.51 the picked creature is an ordinary reference of that creature (`apps/openmw/mwclass/creaturelevlist.cpp`) and
runs its own record's packages (`apps/openmw/mwclass/creature.cpp`); all 86 creatures the island's lists can give
start with Wander.

Separately, the attack-on-sight rule (Fight plus the distance bias reaching 100) ran only in the combat playground,
so even a moving hostile creature would not start a fight on its own.

## Fix

- The builder adds a routes row for every levelled reference (compiled at the largest Wander distance its list can
  give) and a package row for every creature such a list can give; the engine joins the two when the picked creature
  comes near (same AWAM1 layout, two reserved keys). The table grows to 10,176 rows (1.75 MB on disk).
- To keep memory flat at 32 MB Fast, the engine no longer keeps the rows resident: only the first key of every 32
  rows (about 2.5 KB) and the idle tables; a lookup reads one 896-byte block, and an actor found absent is
  remembered.
- Creatures the ambient module moves run OpenMW's engage check (`apps/openmw/mwmechanics/actors.cpp` engageCombat,
  `mechanicsmanagerimp.cpp` isAggressive, `combat.cpp` getFightDistanceBias) through the combat layer's
  `AW_CombatAggro`, about once a second; at most four fight at once. `chim actors ambient aggro off` turns it off.
- Kept by `tests/test_ambient_lut.py` and `tests/aga_ambient_test.c` (levelled rows, the join, the wiring).
- Flying creatures (cliff racers) fly straight to their destinations off the ground, as OpenMW's aiwander.cpp moves
  a pure flyer (ACTOR-MOVING-RUNTIME-GROUND-48).
- Not in this fix: swimming creatures still stand; NPCs do not aggro on sight until their disposition toward the
  player is modelled. Details: [NPC pathfinding](../NPC_PATHFINDING.md), [Combat](../COMBAT.md).
