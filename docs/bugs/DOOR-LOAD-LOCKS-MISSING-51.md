_Last updated with AmiWind v0.0.51 (25 October 2026)._

## Status: 25 October 2026

Open. Left on purpose until a lock can be opened.

## Symptom

Every door into a room opens, whether the original game locks it or not.

## Where

The door use in `engine/aga/src/aw_scene.c` (only the opening's story locks a load door) and the door table, which
carries no lock level or key.

## How it happened

Found on 24 October 2026 while the scripts' `Lock` and `Unlock` were wired into the hinged doors. The load doors
read no lock level (FLTV) or key (KNAM) from the game data.

## Why it is not fixed yet

Honouring the locks now would shut the player out of every locked house: there is no lockpicking, no Open spell and
no key check yet.

## Repair

When locks can be opened: the door table carries each door's lock level and key; using a locked door plays the
locked sound and shows the Locked prompt; a script's lock (`aw_mwhost.c`) wins over the data; a carried key opens it.

## Verification

Pending.
