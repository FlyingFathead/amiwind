## Status: 9 October 2026

Closed as an accepted platform limit, recorded as a fact by owner order. Not a defect to fix: the builder
enforces the limits.

## Symptom

A partition that starts past 2 GiB on its drive is not mounted at boot. The boot failure that showed it
(the full two-disk v0.0.33 build asking for a volume) is recorded under BUILD-WORLD-PARTITION-MOUNT-33.

## Where

Builder disk layout: partition order and start offsets on each drive image (`docs/BUILD_OUTPUT.md`,
`docs/WORLD_STREAMER.md`, the disk-layout gate).

## How it happened

The limits of the platform (Kickstart 3.1 with classic FFS):

- every partition must be smaller than 2 GiB;
- every partition must also START below 2 GiB on its drive: Kickstart 3.1 does not mount a partition that starts
  past 2 GiB;
- files stay well under 2 GiB (PAKs below about 1 GiB, sharded);
- drive images stay below 4 GiB; the target is the whole game on one HDF.

The layout put a small partition after large ones, so one started past 2 GiB although its size was legal.

## Why it was not caught

The disk-layout gate checked partition sizes but not start offsets.

## Reproduction

Build a two-disk layout in which a partition starts at or beyond 2 GiB of its drive and boot it: the volume
requester appears.

## Repair

The builder orders the partitions so each starts below 2 GiB, and a gate stops the build otherwise. No other
repair exists or is needed: the limit belongs to the platform.

## Verification

See BUILD-WORLD-PARTITION-MOUNT-33 for the boot test of the repaired layout; the disk-layout gate and its
self-test (`tools/build.py --layout-selftest`) refuse every layout that breaks one of the four limits.

## Prevention

The disk-layout gate checks all four limits for every image type: partition size, partition start offset, file
size and drive image size.
