## Symptom
The CHIMport re-measure of 18 October 2026 (current builder) fails the stair walk on four cells:

| Cell | Mesh | Failed steps | What the walker found |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- |
| -2,6 (Ald-ruhn) | `x/ex_redoran_steps_02` | 17 of 1,106 | start in solid on the step tops, collision 7 to 14 units above the step |
| -3,12 (Maar Gan) | `x/ex_redoran_steps_02` | 12 of 863 | the same pattern |
| 5,-7 (Suran) | `x/ex_hlaalu_steps_07` | 2 of 244 | blocked by a 26.7 degree collision face 0.8 to 1.5 units over the step |
| 0,8 | `x/ex_ropebridge_2048_01` | 2 of 317 | start in solid, 23 units over the step |

## Where
The CHIM collision of these three meshes (the shared mesh converter); the CHIM stair gate reports it.

## How it happened
Measured on 18 October 2026 (frame builds of the four cells, the converter's pieces read in each mesh's own frame,
and the frames' point and standing hulls traced at the failing starts). All three meshes carry their own collision
mesh (a RootCollisionNode: 154, 84 and 684 triangles under 860, 262 and 3,472 visible ones):

- `ex_redoran_steps_02`: the authored collision of the fan staircase is not steps but a ramp of a few plates (45 to
  50 degrees) from below the bottom step to the top landing, 7 to 14 units over the visible treads. The convex pieces
  copy it faithfully (one 4-triangle piece is one ramp plate), so the standing box on a visible tread starts inside
  it. The earlier reading "the next step's slab closes the space under its nosing" was this ramp. The authored cheek
  walls at the fan's sides also stand inside the visible treads. The stair rule (`mitigate_stairs`) finds no treads
  in a collision mesh that is a ramp, and only reads tread centres.
- `ex_hlaalu_steps_07` (Suran 5,-7): its own collision is a ramp through the nosings (walkable). The walk is blocked by
  the next placement, `ex_hlaalu_canal_12`: its convex proxy of the canal walkway and the wall behind it invents a
  50-degree slope 4 to 8 units over the visible walkway the flight leads onto (no authored triangle lies in it; the
  piece's error measure, triangle centres inside the hull, is near zero, so it was never split).
- `ex_ropebridge_2048_01` (0,8): one convex piece of 260 triangles is the hull of the sagging deck; its top is the
  chord over the sag, 22.8 units over the planks (the deck triangles lie on the hull's boundary, so the same error
  measure stays near zero).
- Found on the way: the CHIM model variants take only the mesh-wide exact-bevel flag, so plates marked for exact
  bevels by position get the approximate hull ([CHIM-STAIR-PLATE-BEVELS-PER-PIECE-43](CHIM-STAIR-PLATE-BEVELS-PER-PIECE-43.md)):
  a leaning visible cheek of the Redoran fan, made a plate, reached 5 units into a start box.

## Why it was not caught
These cells were never CHIMported successfully; the classic maps of the towns walk.

## Reproduction
CHIMport run on cell -2,6, -3,12, 5,-7 or 0,8.

## Repair
One rule in the shared converter (`mesh_geometry.clear_visible_treads`, called by `collision_pieces` with the mesh as
drawn; the CHIM model builder opts in with the profile key `visible_treads`, rooms and the legacy towns do not).
Visible geometry is unchanged:

1. The visible flights of the mesh (`flight_treads`: treads joined by risers at least 16 units wide, three levels
   more than a unit apart) are sampled across their area, where the drawn mesh leaves the player's whole height free.
2. A collision piece in that open column more than 0.5 over a tread, and either more than a step over it or under a
   top face too steep to stand on (the stair rule's own test, now over the visible treads), stands over the flight.
3. Such a piece is split while it has more than four authored triangles (the bridge deck: 7 pieces become 19), then
   made the authored plates of its triangles.
4. An authored plate that still stands there, is not a wall and lies at the flight (three quarters of its plan area
   within the standing box's width of the treads under it) is a ramp the authors ran over the steps: the visible
   flight under it becomes the collision (its treads, risers and the faces down to a standing height under the
   lowest tread), and every other authored piece lying mostly in that flight goes too, its visible cheeks and rails
   taking its place (the Redoran fan: 30 pieces become 176).
5. A mesh this rule changes gets exact standing bevels for every piece (the workaround for
   CHIM-STAIR-PLATE-BEVELS-PER-PIECE-43).
6. The walkways a Hlaalu flight leads onto: the family `ex_hlaalu_canal_*` (`visible_walkways`, set in
   `town_regions.visual_profile`) also has its walkable faces checked, for slopes its convex proxy invented more than
   a step over them (canal_12: 10 pieces become 14). Measured on every exterior mesh: the same walkway test for all
   architecture would change 234 meshes placed in 858 CHIM frames, so it stays limited to this family.

Island-wide (every static mesh of the base archive, 4,076): 64 meshes change, 23 of them exterior; those 23 are
placed in 213 of the 1,298 CHIM frames of the dev2 build.

## Verification
18 October 2026, cell-only frame builds (`tools/chim_build.py --chim-regions-cells ... --validate --stats`, a fresh
unit cache each):

| Run | Frames | Steps and ramps tested | Failed flight steps | Validation | Heap gate |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- | --- |
| The four cells, before the repair | 4 | 2,746 | 32 (17, 11, 2, 2) | passed | passed |
| The 39 stair-held cells (the four and 35 around Ald-ruhn, Buckmoth Legion Fort, Maar Gan and Suran) | 39 | 10,883 | 0 | passed | passed |
| Every region frame of the dev2 build that places a changed mesh (211, and 0,10 enclosed by them) | 212 | 56,051 | 0 | passed | passed |
| The town frames that place one (Seyda Neen, Vivec) | 2 | 19,270 | 0 | passed | passed |

All 213 frames that place a changed mesh passed the stair gate in the dev2 full build before the repair; none fails
after it. The rule is opted into by the CHIM model builder only: the legacy towns and the rooms keep their collision
(test), and of the exterior meshes only the 23 listed by the census change.
Not yet: an orbit clip of a fixed Ald-ruhn flight in the emulator needs an image with these cells on CHIM (the next
full build of the line, or a MiniWind sandbox of region cells, which the builder does not offer yet).

## Prevention
`tests/test_visible_tread_collision.py`: a ramp over a flight is replaced by the flight, a ramp through the nosings
stays, a sagging deck proxy is split, an invented walkway wedge is split for a family with `visible_walkways` only,
table tops and small ledges stay, converters that do not opt in keep their collision. The stair gate stays as it was.
