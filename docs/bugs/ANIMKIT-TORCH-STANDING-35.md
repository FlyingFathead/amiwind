## Status: 10 October 2026

Open. Known in v0.0.35.

## Symptom

With the animation kit on (the default), a guard carrying a torch at night shows it while standing.
When the guard walks or runs, the torch and its light are gone until the guard stops.

## Where

The animation kit's walking and running models have no frames for an attached torch.

## How it happened

The kit added walk and run models; the torch attachment was carried over for the standing models only.

## Why it was not caught

No night check of a walking guard was part of the animation kit's MiniWind presets.

## Reproduction

At night in Balmora, follow a patrolling guard with a torch.

## Repair

Not yet repaired: give the walk and run models the torch attachment, or keep the standing model while a torch is held.

## Verification

Not yet verified.

## Prevention

A MiniWind night preset with a walking torch guard.
