_Last updated with AmiWind v0.0.42-dev1 (17 October 2026)._

## Symptom

The v0.0.42-dev1 full build stopped in the image step, at the guard torches: `Staged NPC lacks original
source identity: vpi015.bsp`. vpi015 is Vivec's palace; the entity is Vivec himself (`vivec_god`).

## Where

`tools/prepare_guard_torches.py`, `_guard_models`: it reads every resident entity of the staged maps and looks
each one up among the master's NPC records to see whether it is a guard who carries a torch.

## How it happened

The interior creature residents (tools/creature_residents.py,
[INTERIOR-CREATURES-MISSING-41](INTERIOR-CREATURES-MISSING-41.md)) place listed creatures as `aw_npc`
entities, Vivec first. Vivec is a creature record, not an NPC record, so the torch guard found no NPC record
for him and refused the map.

## Why it was not caught

The creature residents were tested in their own stage; no test ran the torch guard on a map holding a
creature resident, and the gates build no full image.

## Reproduction

A full build of the v0.0.42-dev1 line before 5db820c, with the default `--interior-creatures listed`.

## Repair

The torch guard also reads the master's creature records and skips a resident whose source is a creature
(creatures carry no torches). A resident whose source is neither an NPC nor a creature is still refused.

## Verification

A test places Vivec beside the refusal case; the fixed step ran on the failed build's own staged maps
(13 guard models, Vivec skipped) before the build was resumed.

## Prevention

The test covers creature residents in the torch guard. Every image-step reader of `aw_npc` entities has to
accept creature residents; the others (actor grounding, ground checks) already read them from the policy.
