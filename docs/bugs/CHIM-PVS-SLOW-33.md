## Status: 8 October 2026

Fixed in source on v0.0.33-chim-format, not shipped at the time of writing; the new time is being
measured. Performance. Found in the CHIM builder's own section timers (build profile) on the
Seyda Neen world.

## Symptom

A Seyda Neen CHIM build where every unit was reused except the visibility rows spent 331 s of its
342 s, using 961 CPU seconds on 3 workers, in "visibility rows". The rows had been built minutes
earlier by an 8-worker build of the same inputs.

## Where

`tools/chim/world.py` (`chunk_visibility`), `tools/chim/ground.py` (`TriangleGround.__call__`).

## How it happened

Two causes:
- The visibility rows are cut into blocks of source chunks, 4 per worker. Each block is a cached
  unit keyed by its chunks, so another `--jobs` made other blocks and none was reused. The rows
  themselves never depended on the worker count.
- Each sight-line sample on irregular ground evaluated every triangle of its 128-unit tile; the
  port's tiles hold 32.

## Why it was not caught

Balmora's regular grid samples in constant time, and its builds always used 8 workers.

## Reproduction

Build Seyda Neen with `--jobs 8`, then again with `--jobs 3`, and read the receipt's `timing`.

## Repair

- A fixed number of blocks (`PVS_BLOCKS`, 32), so the cached units no longer depend on the
  worker count.
- An 8-unit cell lookup: a cell that lies inside one triangle reads that triangle's plane; the
  points in the other cells take the exact path. A test checks the lookup against the exact
  triangles.

## Verification

Pending: the Seyda Neen build time after the change.

## Prevention

Every build's receipt carries the section timers; the perf ledger collects them.
