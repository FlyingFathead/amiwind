## Status: 23 October 2026

Fixed for v0.0.48 on the rc1 line.

## Symptom

The rc1 cache-warming build (23 October 2026) stopped in the actor-contact stage: "Actor placement gate failed: 110
unresolved cases", every one "Scene must declare the converted humanoid standing hull", in Balmora's rooms,
Addamasartus, the trade house and others.

## Cause

Since `--room-form shell` (the rc1 line), the town room stages convert their rooms as shells: a room map with no placed
mesh and no collision of its own, which the image installs as a CHIM room standing on the interior store's models.
The scene stage's early actor check (tools/check_scene_actors.py, before the image) still measured every resident on
its map, so each shell room's residents were refused before any contact was measured.

## Fix

tools/check_actor_ground.py audit and require take `defer_shells`: the early check records a shell room's residents
as `deferred-to-chim-room` after classifying them (identity, support mode, death pose, transform, owner), and the
image's check, which runs without it on the installed CHIM rooms, measures them on the store models
(CHIM-ROOM-SHELL-NO-HULL-48 makes those rooms declare the standing hull). A shell still present at the image is
measured as before and refused. tests/test_room_late_passes.py
test_a_shell_rooms_resident_is_deferred_before_the_image_and_refused_in_it.
