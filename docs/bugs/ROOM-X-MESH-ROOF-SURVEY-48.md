_Last updated with AmiWind v0.0.48-dev2 (23 October 2026)._

## Symptom

Measured while fixing ROOM-PYLON-ROOF-48: of the 209 exterior (`meshes/x/`) models placed in interior cells, 58
have a convex collision piece that stands more than a step (8.5 units) over a walkable face of its own (the Vivec
canton pieces `ex_vivec_h_*`, `ex_vivec_g_*`, Daedric and Dwemer ruins, Redoran steps, rope bridges, `terrain_volcano`
and others). A player or resident on such a floor in a room stands on an invented slope over it.

## Where

The room converter's collision for those meshes (`mesh_geometry.collision_parts`); the list is in
`config/collision-room-roof.json` (`measured_not_listed`).

## How it happened

Same cause as ROOM-PYLON-ROOF-48: the convex conversion's closure measure only sees surface a hull closes at the
triangles' centres. The room converters read the RootCollisionNode of these models since 22 October 2026.

## Why it was not caught

No check measured surface the pieces cover; the v0.0.48 fix listed only the pylon, whose residents failed the actor
ground check.

## Reproduction

Run `mesh_geometry.collision_pieces` with the profile `{'roof_collision_beyond': 8.5}` on each listed mesh.

## Repair

Open. Measure the clip-node cost of the authored plates in every room that places these meshes, then list the meshes
in `config/collision-room-roof.json`.
