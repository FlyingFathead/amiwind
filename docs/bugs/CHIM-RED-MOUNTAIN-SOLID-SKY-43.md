## Status: 18 October 2026

Cause found and fixed in the builder and the engine (branch v0.0.43-redmountain, for v0.0.43-dev4): the CHIM
terrain and the frame maps closed at a fixed height of 2,048 frame-local units, and Red Mountain's ground reaches
4,730. In-game verification waits for a build with the fix.

## Symptom

Walking up Red Mountain, the first-person view breaks apart once the eye passes about 2,048 frame-local units
(global Z about 8,200): the sky shows below the horizon, a slab of terrain hangs in the air and the ground under the
player is not drawn; looking down in noclip shows only the sky. The orbiting third-person camera, lower down, still
shows intact terrain with the player standing on it. A map teleport to Odrosal and the region's player start put the
player inside solid ("Still inside solid", `dbg recover`: "Interior spawn blocked"), and the player can fall through
the ground there.

## Where

Red Mountain and its slopes: cells 2,6, 2,7, 3,7 and 2,9 in the reports; 69 of the 1,298 frames of the v0.0.43-dev2
world have ground over 2,048 (highest 4,730 frame-local, about 18,920 game units, at cells 3,9, 3,8, 4,9 and 4,8).

## How it happened

Every CHIM chunk's terrain closes its node and leaf boxes at the terrain ceiling, a constant of 2,048 frame-local
units taken over from the town converter, where it was the top of the town frame box. The frame map's world bounds
used the same constant, and so did the downward trace that places each region frame map's player start.

- Drawing: Quake's software renderer culls and clips by node boxes. When a node's box lies wholly inside a frustum
  plane, its faces are no longer clipped against that plane. A face above its node's box is then projected without
  clipping: near the eye it lands anywhere on screen. On Red Mountain every face over 2,048 is outside its box, so the
  view breaks apart once the eye nears that height.
- Player start: the region frame map's start is traced down from 2,048. Where the ground is higher (cell 2,9: 2,890 to
  4,700), the trace starts inside the mountain, and the start is inside solid.
- Map teleport (`AW_MapPlace`): the engine searched down from the world bounds top, capped at 3,990 and at 4,000 from
  the narrow-coordinate era. With the bounds at 2,048, a teleport to Odrosal (ground over 2,048) also started inside
  the mountain.

The coordinate range was not the limit: positions travel as 24-bit coordinates of 1/256 units since v0.0.36
(`engine/aga/src/aw_coord.h`, `MSG_WriteCoord` / `MSG_ReadCoord` in `common.c`), up to 32,768; node and leaf bounds
are 16-bit shorts (to 32,767), and collision traces use floats.

## Why it was not caught

Nothing compared the terrain's height with the boxes that hold it. Before v0.0.43-dev2 no CHIM frame had ground over
2,048; the Red Mountain cells joined CHIM in dev2, and the zone walk, stair walk and heap gates never look at bounds.

## Reproduction

In v0.0.43-dev2, climb Red Mountain past global Z of about 8,200 (cell 2,6 at local Z 2,053 in the reports), or
teleport to Odrosal from the map.

## Repair

- `tools/chim/terrain.py` (`frame_ceiling`): a frame whose highest ground comes within 512 units of 2,048
  (`FRAME_HEADROOM`), or whose highest placed model comes within 64 units of it, gets the ceiling of the higher of the
  two plus a 4,096-unit flight band (`FLIGHT_BAND`), rounded up to 1,024 (Red Mountain: ground 4,730, Dagoth Ur's
  surroundings up to 4,858 -> 9,216), for every chunk and for its frame map. A placement is linked into the leaves of
  its chunk, so a leaf box under the model's top would cull the model while its top is in view: in dev2, 23 frames
  under the ceiling had models up to 2,253 over it, a tower on ground of 1,392 among them; a chunk whose own ground comes within 64 units of its
  ceiling is raised the same way (`chunk_ceiling`). Every other frame keeps 2,048, so its units stay byte-identical.
  A chunk whose faces would still lie over its ceiling is refused when it is built, naming the chunk.
- `tools/chim/build.py`: the frame's ceiling from its highest ground (`frame_ground_zmax`) before the chunk tasks.
- `tools/chim/frame_map.py`: the frame map's bounds and the player start trace use the frame's ceiling (`frame_top`).
- `engine/aga/src/aw_spawn.c`: the map placement search starts at the frame's world top within the coordinate range
  (`AW_COORD_BOUND`), not at the old 3,990 / 4,000 clamp; with narrow coordinates it stays 3,990.

A taller box only changes bound values: nodes, leaves, planes, clipnodes, faces and bytes are the same
(`tests/test_chim_terrain_ceiling.py`).

## Verification

The height audit of the v0.0.43-dev2 world (1,298 frames, 260 s on one core): highest ground 4,730 (cell 3,9), highest
placement top 4,858 (cell 2,9); 79 frames have ground within 256 units of 2,048 or over it; 92 frames fail: the 69
with ground over 2,048 (8,386 chunks with ground over their box top and faces outside their node boxes) and 23 more
with placed models over their chunk's box (51 chunks).

Flying high over a frame that keeps 2,048 (`tests/aga_chim_world_test.c` mode `flight`, on the engine's own CHIM
world code): from 6,000 the eye is in an empty leaf whose PVS row sees the same leaves as on the ground, the same
chunks stay active (the ring is by x and y), the same placements stay linked, every face lies inside its node's box
and every upward ground face faces the eye, so the frame's terrain and objects still draw. Only frames whose ground or
models reach the ceiling need the taller box.

Pending: a cell-only build of the Red Mountain cells and an in-game check of the four reported spots (standing on the
ground, terrain drawn, a flight over the summit and its flat neighbours).

## Prevention

- The CHIM validation checks every chunk's faces against its node boxes and its empty leaves' boxes
  (`chim.validate.world_tree`), so a face over its box fails the build's validate step.
- `tools/chim/height_audit.py` audits a world (and with `--maps` an image's frame maps) island-wide: chunk ground
  against node box tops, faces against their boxes, placement tops against their chunk's box, the frame map's top
  against the highest ground plus the headroom, and the player start against the ground under it.
- Tests: `tests/test_chim_terrain_ceiling.py` (ceilings, the start trace, no extra bytes, the validator), a Red
  Mountain map placement case in `tests/aga_spawn_test.c`, the high flight in `tests/aga_chim_world_test.c`, and the
  coordinate audit (`config/coord-range-audit.json`) no longer lists the map placement height as kept.
