## What happens

Ordinary interior doors (a DOOR reference with no destination: a door that swings instead of loading another place)
could not be opened. Only one door, the census office hall door, opened, by its own story rule.

## Reports

- 28 September 2026: first noticed (downstairs interior doors).
- 22 October 2026, v0.0.48-dev2: the maintainer, again: interior doors without a loading screen still do not open or
  close, none of the door types. Seen again, so the bug is persisting.

## Repair

Fixed in source in v0.0.48-dev2: ordinary hinged doors turn 90 degrees in one second about their hinge on the use key,
with the DOOR record's open and close sounds; collision turns with the door; a locked door stays shut and plays the
locked sound; a door holds instead of turning into the player or an actor. Room map edicts and CHIM frame placements.
Keys, lockpicks and the Open spell wait for the inventory and magic work. The in-game check list is
in [Door mapping](../DOOR_MAPPING.md).

23 October 2026, v0.0.48 line (merge of the dev line into the interiors line): CHIM rooms too, the batch rooms
included: a room record's placement whose reference is an ordinary door asks the same hook as the frames when the
room loads and turns with its collision (`chim_room.c` `ChimRoom_DoorYaw`, `ChimRoom_DoorClip`). The census hall door
(172860) is a hinged door now as well: the opening story holds only its lock (the door guard unlocks it once the
player has the papers, as the original's `CharGenDoorGuardTalker` does), and it turns by the same rule as every other
door, which also settles its swing side (DOOR-HALL-SWING-DIRECTION-48). A door that holds against the player says
so once. The worked example and the in-game check, census office first, are in
[Door mapping](../DOOR_MAPPING.md#the-worked-example-the-census-hall-door).
