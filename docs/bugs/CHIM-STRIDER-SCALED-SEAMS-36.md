_Last updated with AmiWind v0.0.44-dev1 (19 October 2026)._

## Status: 10 October 2026

Closed, not a bug. The report was a misunderstanding: the maintainer meant that Balmora's strider is the good one and should be the
base model; the open seams he remembered were Balmora's own, fixed in v0.0.34
([MESH-LOD-OPEN-SEAMS-33](MESH-LOD-OPEN-SEAMS-33.md)). Measured on every CHIM world since v0.0.34: Seyda Neen's `siltstrider@s0.89`
is exactly Balmora's `siltstrider@s1` scaled by 0.89 (3,219 faces, face by face, at most 0.00004 units apart). The check on the way
found two invisible defects, handled separately: vertex sharing per scale (fixed in source) and
CHIM-VARIANT-YAW-DUPLICATES-36. Balmora's strider is now the documented reference model (docs/chim/REFERENCE_MODELS.md).

## Symptom

The silt strider at the Seyda Neen docks shows the open shell seams that
[MESH-LOD-OPEN-SEAMS-33](MESH-LOD-OPEN-SEAMS-33.md) repaired; the strider at Balmora is closed.

## Where

CHIM bakes a placement's scale into its own model variant (Quake entities have no scale). Seyda Neen's strider is
placed at 89 % size and is the variant `siltstrider@s0.89` (464,480 bytes); Balmora's is `siltstrider@s1`
(464,336 bytes). Both come from the same original mesh.

## How it happened

Unknown. Suspected: the closed-hull repair reaches only the scale-1.0 variant, or the scaled variant comes from a
cache entry made before the repair.

## Why it was not caught

The seam audit (the gate added with MESH-LOD-OPEN-SEAMS-33) may check the original mesh and not every baked
variant; to be confirmed.

## Reproduction

Always: look at the silt strider at the Seyda Neen docks, and at the one in Balmora.

## Repair

To do: find which path builds the scaled variant, give every variant the same closed-hull repair, and audit every
baked variant, not only the original meshes.

## Verification

To do: the seam audit over every variant of every mesh; a headlamp-on frame of both striders at the same angle.

## Prevention

The seam audit runs over every baked variant.
