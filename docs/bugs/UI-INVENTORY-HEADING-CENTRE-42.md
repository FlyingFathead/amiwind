_Last updated with AmiWind v0.0.42 (17 October 2026)._

## Symptom

The word "Inventory" sits off the vertical centre of the bar at the top of the
inventory window.

## Where

The inventory window heading (engine/aga/src/aw_worldui.c, `inventory_draw`).

## How it happened

The heading is placed at a fixed offset rather than centred by the drawn
glyphs' height.

## Why it was not caught

No test measures the heading's vertical position.

## Reproduction

Press I and compare the space above and below "Inventory" in its bar.

## Repair

The heading drew its text centred in a 4..16 band that overlaps the window
frame (whose inside starts at 6), by glyph boxes rather than the painted
pixels. Now the title sits in the 6..17 band on the original title bar
(UI-WINDOW-TITLE-BAR-42) and is centred by its painted pixels
(AW_UITextCentred). Branch v0.0.42-hud-title.

## Verification

tests/aga_ui_test.c checks equal space above and below (within one pixel) and
left and right of a centred title; stills with the evidence.

## Prevention

The centring test of UI-WINDOW-TITLE-BAR-42.
