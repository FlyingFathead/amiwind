## Status: 9 October 2026

Open. Cause established; repair not started. Present in every build of the v0.0.33 development line measured
(source b828d64). This is the umbrella cause of the lighting symptom bugs listed under "Bugs in the same
category" and, from other families, SEYDA-LANTERNS-MISSING-31: BALMORA-LAMPS-DIM-31, LAMPS-RANGE-31,
TERRAIN-LIGHT-UNIFORM-32, INTERIOR-BAKE-UNIFORM-32, OPENING-JIUB-LANTERN-32 and LIGHT-FALLOFF-31.

## Symptom

The owner reported on 9 October 2026 that the lantern above Jiub in the opening ship still does not light the
area in the right warm hue. The same pattern shows in every lit scene: baked light has no colour, no shadows and
no original falloff, and exterior lamps give light only at night through runtime dynamic lights.

## Where

Map conversion for interiors, towns and terrain: `tools/prepare_area.py`, `tools/import_town.py`,
`tools/prepare_bounded_world.py`, `tools/rebuild_balmora_region.py`, `tools/prepare_mesh_bsp.py`,
`tools/interior_lighting.py` and the unused `tools/light_sources.py`. Not affected: the runtime night lamps
(engine `aw_lamps.c`, `tools/night_lighting.py`, `night-windows.txt`), which work as designed.

## How it happened

1. `light_sources.entity()` (`tools/light_sources.py:105`) builds Quake light entities (class, style, colour,
   attenuation) from Morrowind LIGH records, but nothing calls it. `docs/LIGHT_SOURCES.md` says "converters not
   yet wired" (plan items 2 and 4).
2. So the Quake light compiler (ericw `light`) finds no lights. Of 604 light logs from the development builds of
   9 October 2026, 602 report "0 lights 0 suns" and 2 (the NPC gallery) report "1 sun". The compiler only applies
   the minimum light: 24 for the interior void boxes (`prepare_area.py:112`) and 100 for terrain
   (`prepare_bounded_world.py:234`, `import_town.py:356`).
3. Interiors: LIGHT records go to the cheaper own bake (`prepare_area.py:74-75`; `interior_lighting.py:60-87`,
   `bake_surface`): one scalar lightmap per face, ambient plus radius falloff, no shadows, no colour (stored in
   `prepare_mesh_bsp.py:221-225` and `:490-495`).
4. Exterior meshes get no lighting at all (`import_town.py:360`, `rebuild_balmora_region.py:59` append without
   lighting); their faces get lightoffset -1 (`prepare_mesh_bsp.py:491`) and draw with the flat `r_ambient`.
   Exterior LIGH records are never baked; at night the engine adds runtime dynamic lights from `lamps.awl` (the
   nearest `aw_lamp_lights` = 2 lamps) and `night-windows.txt`.

## Why it was not caught

No gate checks light counts in baked maps, and the light compiler's "0 lights" line is not read. Each symptom was
investigated and patched on its own path (falloff, range, brightness, one lantern) instead of at the shared layer.

## Reproduction

Build any map with LIGHT records and read its light compiler log: `0 lights 0 suns`. Count the light logs of a
build: all but the NPC gallery report zero lights.

## Repair

Direction (think in Quake first): wire `light_sources.entity()` into the converters so the light compiler bakes
coloured lights with the original attenuation, using lightstyles for flicker, as Quake does for its own light
entities. The own interior bake stays selectable (DON'T DELETE ANY METHOD). Not started.

## Verification

Not yet. After the repair: light logs report the expected light counts per map, and OpenMW A/B at the same poses
(opening ship lantern, a Balmora interior, a town lamp) shows matching hue and falloff, headlamp off.

## Prevention

A builder and validator check that reports the lights baked per map and fails when a map with LIGH records
bakes 0 lights (a warning until the wiring lands), with a regression test.
