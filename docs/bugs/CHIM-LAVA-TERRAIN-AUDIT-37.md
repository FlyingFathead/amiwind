## Symptom

A targeted retry of 25 lava cells passes source collection but all 25 cells stop at CHIM frame checks. The command exits zero despite the failed-cell summary; no successful conversion or playable acceptance is established.

## Where

`tools/chim/validate.py`, standing hull seams and terrain coverage, called by `tools/chim/build.py` before world acceptance.

## How it happened

The standing-hull audit derives the expected floor solely from the terrain heightfield. The converter also creates a solid bed below a shallow lava volume. The coverage audit classifies every upward warp face as water, including lava, and expects it only below the global water level. The source manifest records lava counts but not pool geometry for these checks. These mismatched assumptions are under investigation; individual geometry failures must still be checked.

## Why it was not caught

The source-stage reuse failure prevented this retry from reaching frame validation. Existing lava converter tests did not exercise these two audits against lava-bearing terrain chunks.

## Reproduction

At source commit ba07cb59e0de0ddf796271d60663b00a6f4b54e9, retry a lava cell such as exterior 1,-2. Frame validation reports standing hull solid above the original ground and an unexpected water sample. The 25-cell retry reports zero successful cells and 25 failures.

## Repair

Candidate: record the frame-local pool geometry in the source manifest; derive the standing floor from both terrain and solid lava beds under the player box. Reuse the existing convex polygon clipper and Quake point-hull contents reader. Count exposed liquid surfaces at each sample and check the actual lava contents there; a whole-face centroid is unreliable where a pool crosses sloping ground. Check lava coverage and source height separately. Ordinary ground, water and collision checks remain enabled. The existing AmiQuake liquid mechanism (`SV_PointContents`, water type and level) supplies the contents semantics; no engine change is needed.

## Verification

The actual 25-cell retry reproduces the failure after the separately gated source-stage repair. Four synthetic regressions reproduce the wrong floor, mixed liquid coverage and undetected missing lava. After the candidate correction, these and existing lava converter and ordinary standing-hull tests pass. The first candidate passed 3,944 full-suite tests and native boot smoke, but all 25 real cells still failed its centroid classification. A slope regression reproduced that candidate mistake; the corrected sampling passes 15 targeted tests and converts exterior 1,-2. At corrected source 95db832, all 25 affected cells convert: one cell reused, 24 computed, zero failures. The corrected exact full gate and matched native/reference lava acceptance remain pending. This does not establish lit, native-playtested or released content. No visible game defect or repaired playable release is proved by this converter failure.

## Prevention

Exercise terrain validation with lava beds, water beside lava, overlapping pool footprints, chunk boundaries and deliberate missing or displaced surfaces. Preserve failed-cell summaries independently of process exit status.


## Format audit follow-up, 12 October 2026

Gate 2221 passes 3,945 tests, both host-parity checks, the zero-warning Amiga build and native boot smoke. However, converted is not acceptance: the real-cell receipts still have failed format audits. The world-tree check allows only empty, solid and water and rejects standard Quake `CONTENTS_LAVA` (-5). This is another lava omission in the same terrain validator, under the same bug ID. A new regression reproduces it and checks that unsupported contents remain rejected. The candidate adds only the implemented lava contents to the allowed set; no unsupported contents or failed audit is ignored. Corrected real-cell audits and matched native/reference evidence remain required.


## Converter readback at source 73c8441, 12 October 2026

All 25 affected cells convert and all 25 individual validation reports return `ok: true` with no failures. Nineteen result records are passed; six retain hull-policy holds. Deferred and unmeasured mechanisms remain distinct from passes. This closes the reproduced converter-level lava audit failures in the candidate, while native/reference scene acceptance and shipment remain outstanding. Unknown leaf contents are still rejected by regression.
