## Status: 11 October 2026

Open. PERSISTING, NOT RESOLVED. Found by the maintainer while reading the v0.0.36-dev3 build.

Repaired in source on v0.0.36-image-speed (not shipped): the image step stages the converted voices, effects and videos on the shared worker pool (prepare_media_assets._stage_media_file), each checked by SHA-256 against the media stage's record and hard-linked into the payload (copied only where a link is not possible), rows in catalogue order, a timed phase with counters. The conversion itself already runs in the media stage with its per-file cache by SHA-256. Test: the same payload and rows with one worker or three, staged files are links of the checked outputs.

## Symptom

The image step puts the audio payload into the image one file at a time: 6,447 voices, 717 effects, 18 music files and 17 videos. The build container used about 1.8 of 24 cores meanwhile.

## Where

The image step (tools/build_aga.py image), the audio and video placement part.

## How it happened

Audio placement was written as a plain loop over files, and converted audio is not looked up in the hashed pool by content.

## Why it was not caught

No gate measures cores used per phase, and the phase prints little progress (BUILD-SILENT-PHASES-36).

## Reproduction

Always: build an image with the full audio payload and watch container CPU use during the image step.

## Persisting since

Persisting since [BUILD-IMAGE-SERIAL-32](BUILD-IMAGE-SERIAL-32.md) (v0.0.32-dev1, 8 October 2026, open): the image step on one core. Earlier attempts and why they did not hold:

- [BUILD-IMAGE-SERIAL-32](BUILD-IMAGE-SERIAL-32.md): the per-map passes were made parallel. Audio placement was not part of that repair.
- [BUILD-IMAGE-UNDERUSED-32](BUILD-IMAGE-UNDERUSED-32.md) (8 October 2026, open): listed parts of the image step still serial; fixed one at a time (the cull, drive readback), never the whole step.
- [BUILD-IMAGE-CHIM-MAPS-SERIAL-36](BUILD-IMAGE-CHIM-MAPS-SERIAL-36.md) (open): the same again for CHIM maps.

Each time one phase was fixed and no check kept the others from staying serial.

## Repair

Planned. Already-converted audio comes from the hashed pool and is reused by SHA-256. Conversion and placement run as parallel units in the shared worker pool, with an ordered result. Only changed sources are converted again.

## Verification

To do: identical image bytes with 1 and 24 workers; the phase uses the available cores; an unchanged rebuild converts no audio file again.

## Prevention

A profile gate flags any phase that stays far under its core budget (BUILD-PIPELINE-UNDERUTILIZATION-36).
