_Last updated with AmiWind v0.0.42-dev1 (17 October 2026)._

## Symptom

The v0.0.42-dev1 full build stopped in the image step's payload preflight, before it wrote anything:
`vti011: 257 models, the engine holds 256 (MAX_MODELS)`. vti011 is Vivec, Telvanni Tower.

## Where

The Telvanni canton's room list, config/vivec_telvanni.json, and the check that decides whether an excluded
room may be listed again.

## How it happened

v0.0.41 left Telvanni Tower out for its heap size. The room work of 16 October
([INTERIOR-EXCLUDE-STALE-41](INTERIOR-EXCLUDE-STALE-41.md)) shares same-lit models and turns plants into
sprites, which brought the room's own model slots to 210, inside the 220 budget, so the room was listed again.
The engine's limit is on all model precaches of the map: slot 0, the world, the inline models, the models its
residents and their carried items precache, and the slots the engine keeps for run time. For this room that
total is 257, one over MAX_MODELS (256).

## Why it was not caught

The listing decision used the room's own model slots. The full precache count
([BUILD-BUDGET-ENGINE-LIMITS-35](BUILD-BUDGET-ENGINE-LIMITS-35.md), tools/map_engine_limits.py) runs on the
finished maps in the image step, so it found the room only in a full build. It did its job there: the build
stopped in 14 seconds, before writing the image.

## Reproduction

A full build of the v0.0.42-dev1 line before the room was excluded again.

## Repair

For v0.0.42-dev1 the room is excluded again, with its measured numbers. To list it again, the room needs at
least one precache less: more decorative meshes as sprites, or a section cut at a doorway
(docs/INTERIOR_SECTIONS.md "Interiors over a limit: the order"), and the room work runs the full precache
count before a room is listed.

## Verification

Open. The resumed v0.0.42-dev1 build passes the payload preflight without vti011.

## Prevention

Planned: the room listing runs tools/map_engine_limits.py on the converted room, so a room that fits its own
budget but not the engine's is never listed.
