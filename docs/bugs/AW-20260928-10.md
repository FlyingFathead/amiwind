## Status: 22 October 2026 (load door sounds)

PERSISTING. Seen again in the in-game A/B of the MiniWind Balmora rooms (22 October 2026): every Balmora door is
silent, in today's room maps and in CHIM rooms alike, while a test sound through the same path plays. The load door
sound part is repaired on the v0.0.48 room-passes branch (not in a build yet); door motion is still to do.

Cause: `tools/prepare_door_audio.py` built `id1/door-sounds.txt` from the opening scene's placed-reference links only
(`id1/scene-doors*.txt`): 28 rows, the Seyda Neen and prison ship doors, none of Balmora's 70 door references. The
room stages' door banks (`doors-<room>.txt`) and the CHIM towns' door banks (written after the door-audio stage) were
never read, and the AWSFX1 table (one row per door, at most 256 in the engine) could not have held every door.

Repair (sound): the catalogue lists every load door of the game data (a DOOR reference with a destination: 3,133 in
Morrowind.esm) and the Census hall door, each with its DOOR record's opening and closing SOUN (the door records use
19 sound pairs of 16 sounds). The key, the game's reference number, is what every door bank uses: room maps, CHIM
rooms, CHIM frames, town interiors. The AWSFX2 table stores each pair once and one `<reference> <pair>` row per door;
the engine (`engine/aga/src/aw_door_audio.c`) reads AWSFX2 and AWSFX1, up to 64 pairs and 8,192 doors, and finds a
door by binary search. A cell record holding a deleted reference no longer drops the whole cell from the catalogue.
Tests: `tests/test_door_audio_catalogue.py`, `tests/aga_door_audio_test.c` (AWSFX1, AWSFX2, a bad pair row). To check
in the game: the Balmora doors play their opening and closing sounds, both ways.

## Status: 19 October 2026

PERSISTING: first recorded on 28 September 2026 for the hall door; reported again twice by the maintainer on
19 October 2026 in v0.0.44-dev1. Not fixed yet.

## Symptom

No interior door opens or closes. A door neither swings nor slides and plays no sound; first seen on the hall
door, and in v0.0.44-dev1 in the Tharys Ancestral Tomb and every other interior.

## Where

Every interior door, in every converted interior.

## How it happened

Doors are converted as static scenery. The original game's door records (their model, open and close sounds)
are not turned into anything that can move.

## Why it was not caught

No check in the image step or the boot smoke opens a door; the register entry from September had no report page,
so it stayed out of sight.

## Reproduction

Always: walk up to any interior door and use it.

## Repair

To do. Quake has the mechanism: a door is a brush entity that QuakeC moves (the func_door kind: rotate or slide,
with its own open and close sounds), and collision follows the moving door. The plan is a design note that maps
each Morrowind door record onto it, then the Tharys tomb as the first test room. The interior plan for v0.0.45
lists working doors with the modular interiors ([PLAN-v0.0.45](../PLAN-v0.0.45.md)).

## Verification

To do: a short clip of a door opening and closing with its sound, in the Tharys tomb and one town interior.
