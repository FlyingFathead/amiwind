## Status: 19 October 2026

PERSISTING: first recorded on 28 September 2026 for the hall door; reported again twice by the maintainer on
19 October 2026 in v0.0.44-dev1. Not fixed yet.

## Symptom

No interior door opens or closes. A door neither swings nor slides and plays no sound; first seen on the hall
door, and in v0.0.44-dev1 in the Tharys Ancestral Tomb and every other interior.

## Where

Every interior door, in every converted interior.

## How it happened

Doors are converted as static scenery. The original game's door records (their model, open and close sounds)
are not turned into anything that can move.

## Why it was not caught

No check in the image step or the boot smoke opens a door; the register entry from September had no report page,
so it stayed out of sight.

## Reproduction

Always: walk up to any interior door and use it.

## Repair

To do. Quake has the mechanism: a door is a brush entity that QuakeC moves (the func_door kind: rotate or slide,
with its own open and close sounds), and collision follows the moving door. The plan is a design note that maps
each Morrowind door record onto it, then the Tharys tomb as the first test room. The interior plan for v0.0.45
lists working doors with the modular interiors ([PLAN-v0.0.45](../PLAN-v0.0.45.md)).

## Verification

To do: a short clip of a door opening and closing with its sound, in the Tharys tomb and one town interior.
