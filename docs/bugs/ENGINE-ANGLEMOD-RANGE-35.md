## Status: 10 October 2026

Fixed in source on the v0.0.35 line; ships with v0.0.35.

## Symptom

anglemod(-360) returned 360 instead of 0, and NaN or values past the int range went through an undefined conversion.

## Where

`engine/aga/src/mathlib.c anglemod`.

## How it happened

The negative branch adds one full turn too many when the angle is an exact multiple of 360; nothing checked the input range.

## Why it was not caught

The engine came from the original sources, where these paths assumed well-formed input; no test fed them bad or unusual values.

## Reproduction

Found by reading the source (v0.0.35 engine review).

## Repair

The result is folded into [0,360); NaN and values beyond +-1e9 give 0.

## Verification

Native fixture over exact multiples, small negatives, NaN and huge values (tests/test_engine_review_native.py).

## Prevention

Untrusted input and unusual values are checked where they enter the engine; each fix has a regression check.
