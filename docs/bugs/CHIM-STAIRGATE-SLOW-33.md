## Status: 8 October 2026

Open; performance. Found by the CHIM builder when the stair walkability gate was wired into the CHIM
validator.

## Symptom

The CHIM stair gate (`chim.collision.stair_gate`) checked Balmora's world in 52 s on 4 workers. On
the world of Balmora and the Vivec Arena it ran for over 10 minutes on 4 workers and was stopped
without a result. The timing was taken on a busy host.

## Where

`tools/chim/collision.py` (`stair_gate`, `stair_task`) with the shared walker
`tools/stair_walk.py` (`check_polys`, `walk`).

## How it happened

Not measured. Likely: the Arena has many seat steps, and each walk traces the standing box through
large models' chains of convex pieces. The frame scene also tests every placement's bounds for
every trace.

## Why it was not caught

The gate was first timed on Balmora only.

## Reproduction

Run `require_stairs` on a world built with `--area balmora --area vivec_arena`.

## Repair

Measure where the time goes first: steps per core and traces per step. Candidates:
- a spatial index for the frame scene's brush filter;
- smaller cores where the steps are dense;
- caching traces across the steps of one flight.

## Verification

Pending: the gate's time on the Arena world.

## Prevention

The gate's time per frame goes into the build profile.
