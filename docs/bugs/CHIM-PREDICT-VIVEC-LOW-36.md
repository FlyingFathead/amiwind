## Status: 10 October 2026

Open. Found by the Vivec on CHIM preflight (milestone M3, phase 1), a read-only comparison of the predictor with
the heap gate of the Vivec district frames built on 9 October. Until it is refitted, Vivec memory is decided by
built heap gates only.

## Symptom

The predictor (`tools/chim/predict.py`) estimates the ring bytes the engine's zone must hold at any position
before anything is built. For the eight Vivec district frames it is far too low:

- with the large-model cut approximated (triangles spread evenly over a cut model's footprint), 28-54 % below
  the active-ring peak the heap gate measured on the built frames (Temple 4.33 MB predicted, 8.25 MB built);
- with every model whole, 18-39 % below the cut builds, which hold less than whole models, and 49 % below the
  one build without the cut (the Arena: 3.32 MB predicted, 6.49 MB built, over the 6.24 MB chunk room).

The predicted peaks take every placement of the whole city, so they are if anything high for one district frame.

The design notes said the Vivec Arena "fits by both opinions"; the built frame did not fit until the large-model
cut (CHIM-ARENA-MEMORY-33).

## Where

`tools/chim/predict.py`: one linear cost per model, fitted on two built worlds (Balmora and Seyda Neen): about
88.6 bytes per source triangle plus 6.4 KB per model. Measured decoded blocks of Vivec meshes on 9 October:

| Mesh | Source triangles | Decoded block | Bytes per triangle | Built / predicted |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| `ex_v_vivecstatue_02` | 5,451 | 726,688 | 133 | 1.49 |
| `ex_v_vivecstatue_01` | 4,009 | 526,064 | 131 | 1.46 |
| `ex_v_stolms_01` | 3,395 | 450,704 | 133 | 1.47 |
| `ex_v_dock_01` | 2,514 | 316,928 | 126 | 1.38 |
| `ex_gondola_01` | 1,024 | 138,496 | 135 | 1.43 |
| `ex_vivec_b_tb_01` | 1,450 | 315,904 | 218 | 2.34 |
| `ex_vivec_b_t_01` | 1,122 | 220,272 | 196 | 2.08 |

The canton bodies are further off: one whole canton body (`ex_vivec_c_04`) was a 2,514,432-byte block for 6,166 source triangles
(408 bytes per triangle).

## How it happened

The predictor is a draft fitted on the two towns built at the time. Vivec's architecture has large faces that
the surface-extent limit splits into many faces, many materials per mesh and long collision piece lists, so it
decodes to more bytes per source triangle than Balmora's houses and Seyda Neen's shacks.

## Why it was not caught

Its check (`predict.py check`) compares only worlds it was fitted on (leave-one-out over Balmora and Seyda
Neen). No built Vivec world was in the check, and the second opinion from placed face counts was not compared
with a built Vivec frame either.

## Reproduction

Always: run `predict.py map` (or the same ring sum over a frame) with the fitted coefficients over the Vivec
district frames and compare the peaks with the heap gate of the same frames built by the CHIM builder.

## Repair

To do: refit with the Vivec frames in the fit (and a per-mesh class or a face-count term for large flat
architecture), and add the Vivec frames to the leave-one-out check. Until then the M3 plan uses built heap gates
only.

## Verification

To do: the refitted predictor within its stated error on the Vivec frames in leave-one-out.

## Prevention

The predictor's check must include every town type it is used to plan, and a plan step that relies on it states
the error measured on that type.
