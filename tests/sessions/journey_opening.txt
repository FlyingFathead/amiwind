# The standard gameplay journey (GAMEPLAY-JOURNEY-SMOKE-66; docs/GAME_RECORDER.md "Gameplay journeys").
# A FULL image, FS-UAE at 32 MB Fast, started with --map seyda. Every check reads the game's state (console answers,
# the state file), never a picture. Part A is New Game through the opening as a player plays it: the ship, the pier,
# the clerk's registration, the papers off the table, the hall guard and his door, the back room (a take), out to the
# courtyard and back in (the take stays taken), Fargoth's ring (the Engraved Ring of Healing, `ring_keley`), Sellus
# Gravius (his duties topic chosen by its name),
# the release through the exit door, a kill, a room and back, a spell and a fight, then the game's own quicksave;
# part B is a fresh engine that loads that save through the Load game window and goes on through a door.
# JOURNEY-WALK-DIALOGUE-DESYNC-66: the walk waits for the game and stops at the first wrong turn (tools/engine_smoke.py
# WALKER steps: walkto, waitfor, boxes, mark/saw, topic, face, aimed); the earlier key counts pressed Enter into the
# clerk's persuasion list on the v0.0.66-dev2 image. Map-local points are the maps' own: the census office from its
# entities and doors-census.txt (room units = the original's / 4), the courtyard and the street from doors-seyda.txt
# and the courtyard barrel's frame-map entity (reference 172851). Item ids and the reward are the game's own
# (engine/aga/src/aw_state.c AW_CaptainDuties: the directions, the package and 87 gold, A1_1_FindSpymaster 1).
