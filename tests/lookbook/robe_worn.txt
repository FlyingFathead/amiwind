# Look book page ROBE-WORN (tests/lookbook/pages.json; docs/lookbook/ROBE-WORN.md): a common robe put on shows on the
# body in third person and on the arms in first person (ROBE-PART-NOT-BAKED-62, confirmed by the maintainer on the
# v0.0.67-dev2 image). The numbers: the robe is the one worn (aw_inventory worn), the third-person body is assembled
# from what is worn and the first-person arms from parts (aw_camera's status lines); the pictures beside them.
cmd aw_inventory wear common_robe_01
wait 4
probe robe_worn aw_inventory worn
expect has robe_worn common_robe_01
cmd aw_camera third
wait 4
probe robe_body aw_camera
expect has robe_body Assembled body:
expect lacks robe_body Assembled body: off
pick robe_parts robe_body after Assembled body:
expect robe_parts >= 2
still robe-third
cmd aw_camera first
wait 3
cmd aw_aim 0 50
wait 2
probe robe_arms aw_camera
expect has robe_arms First-person arms:
expect lacks robe_arms the baked arms
still robe-first-down
cmd aw_inventory unequip common_robe_01
wait 2
cmd aw_aim 0 0
wait 1
