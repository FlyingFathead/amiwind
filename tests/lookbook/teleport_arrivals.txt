# Look book page TELEPORT-ARRIVALS (tests/lookbook/pages.json; docs/lookbook/TELEPORT-ARRIVALS.md): a teleport to a
# settlement of the picker's table arrives standing on its ground. Per place: the teleport (aw_teleport, the dbg tp
# word's command), the arrival in play, then the height twice 4 seconds apart (aw_pos: "position X Y Z / movement M";
# a fall or a push out of the ground changes it) and the standing box against the solids (aw_blockers: "solid 1/1" is a
# box inside a solid). The six release smoke hops less Seyda Neen, where the session starts.
cmd aw_teleport balmora
wait 30
boxes
waitfor screen 90 game
wait 6
probe bal_pos0 aw_pos
pick bal_z0 bal_pos0 before / movement
wait 4
probe bal_pos1 aw_pos
pick bal_z1 bal_pos1 before / movement
expect bal_z1 >= bal_z0-2
expect bal_z1 <= bal_z0+2
probe bal_box aw_blockers
expect lacks bal_box solid 1/1
still arrive-balmora
